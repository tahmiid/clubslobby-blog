# Pro Clubs HQ Brand Guide

## Logo system

- **Primary:** white HQ (`#F9FFFE`) over the Emerald Aurora ball, especially on dark interfaces.
- **Alternate:** deep black HQ (`#071821`) over the same ball, for light interfaces and print.
- Keep the ball, motif placement, HQ geometry, and HQ-to-ball scale unchanged.
- Do not rotate, distort, outline, recolor, or add extra effects to HQ.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Emerald | `#2BCB8C` | Highlight side of ball |
| Teal | `#078D86` | Midtone and web theme |
| Blue | `#1B51B6` | Cool depth side of ball |
| HQ White | `#F9FFFE` | Primary lettering |
| HQ Black | `#071821` | Alternate lettering |
| Night | `#051019` | Preferred dark field |
| Mist | `#E8EEF2` | Preferred light field |

## Minimum size and clear space

- Full static logo: minimum 16 px for favicon use; 32 px or larger is preferred in UI.
- Animated loader: minimum 48 px; 64–128 px is the normal UI range.
- Keep clear space equal to at least 8% of the logo diameter around the circle.
- At 16–32 px the football motif is intentionally secondary; HQ remains the recognition anchor.

## Motion

- Approved loop length: 4 seconds.
- Sequence: surge, settle, second kick, settle.
- HQ and its shadow remain screen-locked while the complete shaded ball rotates behind them.
- For reduced-motion users, replace animation with the corresponding 512 px static PNG.

## File selection

- SVG/PDF: presentations, print, layout, and scalable web placement.
- PNG: app icons, UI, social graphics, and transparent raster use.
- Static WebP: compact web imagery.
- Animated WebP: recommended loading animation.
- APNG: lossless transparent animation.
- WebM: transparent video-capable UI.
- GIF: legacy or messaging compatibility; supplied on the intended background.
- MP4: video and CMS surfaces; supplied on the intended background.
