## Directional Nutmeg  `fc27-how-to-directional-nutmeg` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a108.html)
- actions: Directional Nutmeg [1 Star Moves, 1★] `Hold L1 + R1 + R Direction`
- **what:** Push the ball through a defender's legs and choose which way it comes out. The exit is yours, so the ball lands where your next stride is going rather than straight ahead.
- **when:** A defender square on with their feet apart, close enough that a normal touch would hit them. Send the exit to the side they are leaning away from and you are past before they can turn. If the legs close, the ball is theirs.
- **who:** Spark and Magician in one-on-ones out wide, and any forward who runs at a planted full-back. At one star it is not a build decision.
- **note:** Flair Nutmegs is the four-star showpiece version of the same idea.

## Open Up Fake Shot  `fc27-how-to-open-up-fake-shot` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a109.html)
- actions: Open Up Fake Shot Left [1 Star Moves, 1★] `Hold L1 + S or O then X + L ALT`; Open Up Fake Shot Right [1 Star Moves, 1★] `Hold L1 + S or O then X + L ART`
- **what:** A fake shot that opens the body out as the fake finishes, so you come out of it facing diagonally forward instead of square to the defender.
- **when:** Cutting in from the wing with a defender showing you one way. The fake makes them check; the open-up takes you across their front foot into the shooting lane. Use it where a plain fake shot would leave you facing the touchline.
- **who:** Finisher and Spark cutting in to shoot. Any wide forward with a strong far-post finish will get the most out of the exit angle.
- **note:** Mirrored pair: Open Up Fake Shot Left and Open Up Fake Shot Right. Pick the side that opens you onto your stronger foot.

## Flick Up for Volley  `fc27-how-to-flick-up-for-volley` — draft (wave 2, a110.html)
- actions: Flick Up for Volley [1 Star Moves, 1★] `R3`
- **what:** Flick the ball up off the ground so the next touch is a volley rather than a ground strike. Nothing about it beats a defender; it changes what kind of shot or pass you can play next.
- **when:** On the edge of the box with time and nobody closing. The volley that follows is a different shape of shot to a ground strike, and a lofted volleyed pass clears a packed back line. With a defender a stride away it is a giveaway.
- **who:** Finisher and Target, who get the ball on the edge of the box with a yard of space. A Creator can use it to lift a pass over a line.
- **note:** The flick-up is the whole move. What follows is an ordinary volley, whether you shoot it or pass it.

## First Time Feint Turn  `fc27-how-to-first-time-feint-turn` — draft (wave 2, a111.html)
- actions: First Time Feint Turn [1 Star Moves, 1★] `Hold L1 + R1 + L AB`
- **what:** A feint and turn played on the first touch. Instead of controlling the pass and then moving, you sell one direction as the ball arrives and take it off the other way.
- **when:** Receiving with a marker tight on your back or shoulder. Waiting for the ball to settle lets them get a foot in; doing it on the first touch means the ball is moving before they have set. It needs the pass to reach you cleanly.
- **who:** Target and Maestro, who receive with their back to play more than anyone. A Creator dropping in to collect gets the same use out of it.
- **note:** A single action with no left or right version.

## Feint Forward and Turn  `fc27-how-to-feint-forward-and-turn` — draft (wave 2, a112.html)
- actions: Feint Forward and Turn [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Flick R AB AB`
- **what:** Sell a touch forward, then turn back the way you came with the ball. The whole thing happens on the spot, so you end up facing the opposite direction with the ball at your feet.
- **when:** A defender coming at you from the front who has to guess whether you are going past them. The forward feint asks the question; the turn takes the ball away from their answer. You finish facing the way you came, so know what is behind you first.
- **who:** Maestro and Recycler holding the ball under a press, and a Progressor who has carried it into traffic and needs a way out.
- **note:** Not the same as Drag Back, which pulls the ball back without selling the touch forward first.

## Body Feint  `fc27-how-to-body-feint` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a113.html)
- actions: Body Feint Left [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Flick R AL`; Body Feint Right [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Flick R AR`
- **what:** Drop a shoulder and shift your weight one way without touching the ball, then carry on. The ball never moves; only you do.
- **when:** Running at a defender who is backing off, or a step away from one who has stopped. The feint is small and quick, so it is the one you can afford to throw twice. Follow it with a real touch the other way or it is just a wobble.
- **who:** Everyone who carries the ball, but Spark and Magician out wide most of all, and a Progressor stepping out from the back.
- **note:** Mirrored pair: Body Feint Left and Body Feint Right. The name is the direction of the feint, not the direction you go afterwards.

## Stepover  `fc27-how-to-stepover` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a114.html)
- actions: Stepover Left [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Rotate R RTL`; Stepover Right [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Rotate R RTR`
- **what:** Swing a foot around the front of the ball without touching it, then push off. It threatens a change of direction that may or may not come.
- **when:** Running at a defender who is retreating, ideally one who is jockeying rather than diving in. The stepover gives them something to react to; the touch that follows goes wherever they are not. Chain two before the exit if one is not enough.
- **who:** Spark and Magician running at full-backs, and any wide forward who needs a defender to commit before the exit.
- **note:** Mirrored pair: Stepover Left and Stepover Right, one for each side of the ball. Reverse Stepover is the same swing the other way around.

## Reverse Stepover  `fc27-how-to-reverse-stepover` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a115.html)
- actions: Reverse Stepover Left [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Rotate R RLT`; Reverse Stepover Right [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Rotate R RRT`
- **what:** A stepover with the foot travelling the opposite way around the ball. Same fake, opposite swing, which is enough to change what the defender thinks they saw.
- **when:** The same spots as the stepover, running at a retreating defender. Bring it in when the ordinary stepover has stopped working on the person in front of you, or mix the two so the swing is never the same twice.
- **who:** Spark and Magician who already lean on the stepover, and a Finisher cutting in from the flank who wants a second version of it.
- **note:** Mirrored pair: Reverse Stepover Left and Reverse Stepover Right. Same star tier as the ordinary Stepover, so there is no reason to learn one and not the other.

## Ball Roll  `fc27-how-to-ball-roll` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a116.html)
- actions: Ball Roll Left [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Hold R AL`; Ball Roll Right [2 Star Moves, 2★] `Hold R AR`
- **what:** Roll the sole of the boot across the top of the ball to move it sideways a yard. No fake in it; the ball actually goes where the foot sends it.
- **when:** Shifting the ball off a defender's line without turning your body: to open a passing angle, get it onto your stronger foot or step around a slide. It is quick and it repeats, so it is the move you use most and notice least.
- **who:** Every outfield build, above all a Maestro or Creator moving the ball a yard to open a pass, and a Finisher rolling it onto the shooting foot.
- **note:** Mirrored pair: Ball Roll Left and Ball Roll Right. Ball Roll Cut, Ball Roll Drag and Lane Change are all built on it, so it is worth getting clean first.

## Drag Back  `fc27-how-to-drag-back` — PUBLISHED (wave 1, a117.html)
- actions: Drag Back [2 Star Moves, 2★] `L1 + R1 + Flick L AB`
- **what:** Pull the ball back under your body with the sole, away from the defender in front of you. The ball and you stop; the defender usually does not.
- **when:** A defender closing at pace, or a tackle you can see coming. The drag takes the ball out of the challenge and leaves you on it with time to pick the next touch. It also stops you, so it resets the situation rather than winning it.
- **who:** Maestro and Recycler in the middle third, and a Boss when the striker comes to press.
- **note:** Plain drag back, no turn. Drag Back Turn and Drag Back Spin are the higher-star versions that add an exit.
