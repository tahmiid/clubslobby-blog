First Time Spin is one of the skill moves new to EA FC 27, confirmed from the rumor mill. It is a 5-star move, so any pro with 5 skill stars or more can perform it.

Hold +
★★★★★  ·  5-star move

What it does

Spin away from your marker on the first touch, before the ball has settled.

When to use it

Receiving a pass with a defender tight behind you. It is the only move on this list that happens *during* the first touch rather than after it.

Which builds it suits

Five-star strikers who play with their back to goal.

Worth knowing

Two shoulder buttons and nothing else — the simplest input of any five-star move in the game.

5-star requirement

Can your pro do this?

Skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other. Price the jump to 5 stars on your own build before you commit to it.
