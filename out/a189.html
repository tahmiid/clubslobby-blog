Pace winger — outside the full-back, to the byline

The pace winger beats his full-back on the outside and gets the cross or the cut-back in before the defence has reset. Acceleration and sprint speed are the whole point; the finish from a tight angle is the bonus.

  • Archetypes in this role: Spark ×28
  • PlayStyles they carry most: Quick Step, Rapid, First Touch and Finesse Shot
  • What they max out: Acceleration, Curve, Sprint Speed and Dribbling
  • AcceleRATE: Explosive 61% · Controlled 39%

Skill winger — inside, onto the strong foot

The skill winger comes inside. Dribbling, agility and balance let him take the full-back on either way, and the end product is a curled shot from the edge of the box as often as a cross. Right-footed on the left, left-footed on the right — the builds assume it.

  • Archetypes in this role: Magician ×29
  • PlayStyles they carry most: Trickster, Technical, First Touch and Finesse Shot
  • What they max out: Balance, Composure, Ball Control and Acceleration
  • AcceleRATE: Explosive 97% · Controlled 3%

Frequently asked questions

Which archetype makes the best winger in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

On the launch-week meta board Xavi Simons (Creator · 72.7) leads the wide midfielder slot. Across the 57 winger builds in the catalog the archetypes used are Magician (29) and Spark (28). Pick the job first; the archetype follows from it.

Are these Pro Clubs builds or Ultimate Team?

Pro Clubs only. Archetypes, PlayStyle+ slots, specializations and the level-40 cap belong to Clubs; Ultimate Team has none of them. Every card opens the build in the Pro Clubs HQ builder, where you can copy it and change any attribute.

How many ability points does a level-40 winger have?

Every FC 27 pro reaches level 40 with 962 ability points, one signature PlayStyle+ slot and three regular PlayStyle slots. Each build here spends exactly that budget; copy one and the builder re-prices any change live.

How is the ranking decided?

By how many players have copied each build into their own club, then by views, read from the live database on 22 September 2026. Only house-account builds are listed; members' own builds and copies are left out.
