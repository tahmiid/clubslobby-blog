Level 40 is the FC 27 cap: 962 AP to spend on the way from the start to the ceiling. Meta board: the best placing on the FC 27 Season 1 boards (4-2-1-3), each a top ten.

FC 27 has two goalkeeper archetypes: the Shot Stopper and the Sweeper Keeper. Both reach 99 in all five goalkeeping attributes and start level in three of them; the differences are GK Handling (a new Shot Stopper starts at 75, a Sweeper Keeper at 65) and GK Kicking (a new Shot Stopper starts at 65, a Sweeper Keeper at 75). Highest ceilings: the Sweeper Keeper for Pace (90), Physical (96), Passing (87), Ball Control (91), Defending (87) and Scoring (73); Goalkeeping is shared at the top. You have 962 AP to spend at level 40, and each archetype prices every attribute on its own four tiers, so the same upgrade costs a different amount on each.

The same prices for all thirteen archetypes: FC 27 AP costs, every archetype compared.

What each one costs to upgrade

Every archetype has four attributes in its cheapest price tier, and they differ:

  • Shot Stopper: Acceleration, GK Pos., Reactions and Short Pass.
  • Sweeper Keeper: GK Kicking, Long Pass, Vision and Ball Control.

The widest gap between the two: 90 GK Kicking costs 65 AP on a Sweeper Keeper and 234 on a Shot Stopper, each from its own starting value. Raised to 90, the Shot Stopper is the outright cheapest in five of the 32 attributes both carry and the Sweeper Keeper in 12; in one the lowest price is shared; and in 14 at most one of them can reach 90.

Shot Stopper

The Shot Stopper shares the top Goalkeeping ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Acceleration, GK Pos., Reactions and Short Pass; its most expensive tier holds Dribbling, Finishing, Stand Tackle, GK Reflexes, Sprint Speed, GK Kicking, Interceptions and Agility. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the two for Acceleration, Reactions, Short Pass, GK Handling and GK Pos.

  • Inspired by: Gianluigi Buffon. Height: 5'10" to 6'6".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Far Reach. Perks: Low Shot Saver and Ready to Act.
  • Stars: 1★ to 2★ skill moves (5 AP), 1★ to 3★ weak foot (30 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Shot Stopper+, 343 AP (Jumping 90, GK Handling 92 and GK Pos. 90).
  • Recommended roles: Goalkeeper.
  • FC 27 meta boards: no. 1 at goalkeeper (92.2).
  • Most copied Shot Stopper build: The Octopus.

Sweeper Keeper

The Sweeper Keeper has the group’s highest Pace, Physical, Passing, Ball Control, Defending and Scoring ceilings and shares the top Goalkeeping ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are GK Kicking, Long Pass, Vision and Ball Control; its most expensive tier holds Finishing, Dribbling, Acceleration, GK Pos., Interceptions, Stand Tackle, Sprint Speed, Agility, GK Handling and Short Pass. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the two for Agility, Balance, Composure, Vision, Long Pass, Interceptions, Jumping, Strength, Stamina, Aggression, GK Kicking and GK Reflexes.

  • Inspired by: Edwin van der Sar. Height: 6'0" to 6'7".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Footwork. Perks: Back Option and Rush Specialist.
  • Stars: 1★ to 3★ skill moves (15 AP), 1★ to 4★ weak foot (48 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Launcher, 275 AP (Long Pass 90, GK Kicking 92 and GK Reflexes 90).
  • Recommended roles: Sweeper Keeper.
  • FC 27 meta boards: 5th at goalkeeper (86.5).
  • Most copied Sweeper Keeper build: Manuel Neuer.
