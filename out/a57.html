Flair Roulette is one of the skill moves new to EA FC 27, confirmed from the rumor mill. It is a 4-star move, so any pro with 4 skill stars or more can perform it.

PlayStation

Xbox

★★★★☆  ·  4-star move

What it does

The roulette, with the flair animation and a slightly wider turning arc.

When to use it

The same situations as the normal roulette — running into contact — but the wider arc clears a second defender the standard one does not.

Which builds it suits

Players with the flair trait; the animation is slower, so it needs the space the extra arc buys.

4-star requirement

Can your pro do this?

Skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other. Price the jump to 4 stars on your own build before you commit to it.
