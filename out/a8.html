What the thresholds actually gate

You can level any attribute your archetype allows, but only up to its ceiling — and the ceiling is what decides a PlayStyle, because no amount of AP pushes past it. The surprise in the data is how rarely that bites: 16 of the 36 PlayStyles are reachable by every single archetype, keepers included, and 9 of the 13 archetypes tie for the widest menu at 31 PlayStyles each.

The gates that do exist are sharp ones. The 5 Goal Keeping PlayStyles that demand a goalkeeper stat are keepers-only by construction — outfield archetypes simply have no GK attributes to level. And the two keepers pay for their speciality the other way round: low outfield ceilings hold the Shot Stopper to 21 PlayStyles and the Sweeper Keeper to 26, the two narrowest menus in the game.

Jockey and Anticipate are the two real dividers. Both stack Def. Aware and Stand Tackle at 75–80, and that double defensive demand is exactly what the two pure forwards — Finisher and Target — gave away for their scoring ceilings. Everything else on the list is only out of a keeper's reach.

One curiosity going the other way: 1v1 Close Down is filed under Goal Keeping, but its thresholds (Acceleration ≥ 80, Agility ≥ 75, Aggression ≥ 80) name no goalkeeper stat — on the numbers alone it is the one Goal Keeping PlayStyle every archetype's ceilings clear.

Signature PlayStyles are a separate track

Each archetype also carries four signature PlayStyles of its own — select an archetype in the tool and they light up. Those four are baked into the archetype's identity in the catalog, alongside the unlockable pool the thresholds govern. How many PlayStyle slots you have to equip any of this into is a levelling question, not a threshold one — the slot schedule is in our level rewards guide.

Frequently asked questions

What are PlayStyle requirements in Pro Clubs?

Attribute thresholds recorded against each PlayStyle — for example Acceleration ≥ 80. The catalog lists 99 thresholds across the 36 PlayStyles, most commonly two or three per PlayStyle.

Can every archetype unlock every PlayStyle?

No, but it is closer than you would guess. Most outfield archetypes reach 31 of 36; Finisher and Target miss only Jockey and Anticipate; the keepers reach 21 and 26 because their outfield ceilings are the lowest in the game.

Do goalkeepers get their own PlayStyles?

5 of the 6 Goal Keeping PlayStyles require goalkeeper attributes, which only the Shot Stopper and Sweeper Keeper have — outfield archetypes cannot meet those requirements at all. The odd one out is 1v1 Close Down, whose recorded thresholds are all outfield stats.

Does levelling past a threshold do anything extra?

The thresholds are unlock bars, not scaling curves — the catalog records a single minimum per attribute. What extra points cost you is a different question entirely, covered in our AP cost guide.
