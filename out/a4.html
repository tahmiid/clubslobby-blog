FC 27 gives every pro one of three acceleration types, and four numbers decide which: height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration. Explosive is for short pros with Agility well clear of Strength; Lengthy is for tall ones with Strength clear of Agility; everything else is Controlled. 12 of the 13 archetypes can be Explosive and all 13 can be Lengthy. How FC 27 decides your type

The game checks the rules in order and stops at the first one your pro passes:

  1. Explosive: 182 cm or shorter, Agility 65+, Agility 10+ over Strength, Acceleration 80+.
  2. Lengthy: 183 cm or taller, Strength 65+, Strength 4+ over Agility, Acceleration 40+.
  3. Controlled: every pro that fails both.

The gap matters more than the numbers. Agility 95 is not Explosive if Strength is 86; the rule wants Agility at least 10 higher. Lengthy asks for a smaller gap, 4 points of Strength over Agility. Sprint Speed plays no part in either rule.

Height is a hard line. In feet and inches, 5'11" (180.3 cm) is the tallest Explosive height and 6'1" (185.4 cm) the shortest Lengthy one. 6'0" (182.9 cm) passes neither height check, so a 6'0" pro is Controlled whatever its attributes. The Sweeper Keeper starts at 6'0", so it can never be Explosive.

These thresholds are the ones FC 26 used, carried into FC 27. All 13 archetypes’ default types in FC 27, read in the game, agree with them: Target, Boss, Shot Stopper and Sweeper Keeper start Lengthy, the other nine Controlled.

The cheapest way to each type

Explosive is cheapest on the Marauder, 15 AP (Acceleration 80), and dearest on the Boss, 228 AP, because the Boss starts with Strength 75 and needs Agility 85 to clear it by 10. Lengthy costs nothing on six archetypes and at most 62 AP (the Spark). You have 962 AP at level 40, so neither type is out of reach on cost; height is what rules one out.

In the menu vs in a match

FC 27 reads your type twice. The menu uses the attributes you bought. In a match, your height and weight shift Acceleration, Agility and Strength (on a keeper, Acceleration and Strength) for free, along with a few attributes the rules do not read, and the type is read again from the shifted values. On an outfield player, shorter and lighter adds Acceleration and Agility and takes away Strength, which is the Explosive direction; taller and heavier does the opposite.

So the two can disagree. A new Marauder at 5'6" and 143 lb has Agility 70, Strength 60 and Acceleration 75: Controlled in the menu, because Explosive needs Acceleration 80. Its body adds 5 Agility and 5 Acceleration and takes 5 Strength, so in a match it is Agility 75 against Strength 55 with Acceleration 80: Explosive. Four archetypes play Explosive for free that way at their shortest and lightest: Finisher, Magician, Spark and Marauder.

Frequently asked questions

What is AcceleRATE in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

How your pro gets up to speed. There are three types: Explosive (short bursts of speed; accelerates fastest over the first few metres), Lengthy (slow to start but very hard to catch once at top speed) and Controlled, the default for any pro that meets neither. The game decides from four numbers: height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration.

How do you get Explosive in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

Be 182 cm or shorter (5'11" and under), with Agility 65+, Agility at least 10 above Strength, and Acceleration 80+. The cheapest archetype to make Explosive is the Marauder: 15 AP (Acceleration 80).

How do you get Lengthy in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

Be 183 cm or taller (6'1" and up), with Strength 65+, Strength at least 4 above Agility, and Acceleration 40+. Four archetypes are Lengthy from the start (Target, Boss, Shot Stopper and Sweeper Keeper); the dearest to convert is the Spark, 62 AP.

Which archetypes cannot be Explosive?

Only the Sweeper Keeper: its shortest height, 6'0", is above the 182 cm limit. Every other archetype can be Explosive with the right attributes, and every archetype can be Lengthy.

Why does my pro say Controlled in the menu but play Explosive?

The menu reads the attributes you bought. In a match, your height and weight shift Acceleration, Agility and Strength (on a keeper, Acceleration and Strength) for free, measured from the middle of your archetype’s range, and the type is read again. A new Marauder at 5'6" and 143 lb is Controlled in the menu and Explosive in a match. The builder shows both, as “Controlled (in-game Explosive)”.
