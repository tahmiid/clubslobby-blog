What's cheap on a Magician

Four attributes sit in the Magician's cheapest tier, and for three of them no outfield archetype pays less to reach 90: Balance (75 to 90 for 65 AP), Volleys (72 to 90 for 72 AP) and Shot Power (70 to 90 for 76 AP). Composure costs 72 AP from 72; only the Progressor pays less.

The cheap tier holds ten more, priced to 90: Att. Position (71 AP from 80); Ball Control, Curve, Penalties, Reactions and Vision (92 AP each from 75); FK Acc. and Stamina (119 AP each from 65); Aggression (131 AP from 55); Jumping (stops at 85, 83 AP).

What's expensive on a Magician

Eight attributes are in the Magician's most expensive tier. Two are defensive (Stand Tackle and Interceptions); the other six are Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing and Short Pass (177 AP each from 75), Agility (197 AP from 72) and Sprint Speed (209 AP from 70).

Pace is the big bill: Acceleration and Sprint Speed both to 90 cost 386 AP, 40% of your 962. And past 90 every tier gets steep. On this one, points 91 and 92 cost 20 AP each, 93 and 94 cost 25, then 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 for each point from 95 to 99 — so Agility from 72 to its cap of 99 is 487 AP, more than half your budget for one attribute.

Magician vs Spark, Finisher, Maestro and Disruptor

Each archetype puts each attribute in its own price tier, so the same upgrade can cost more than three times as much on one archetype as on another. Where the others pay less than a Magician:

  • Sprint Speed: 90 costs 76 AP on a Finisher or Disruptor, 209 on a Magician.
  • Dribbling: 90 costs 65 AP on a Spark, 177 on a Magician.
  • Short Pass: 90 costs 65 AP on a Maestro, 177 on a Magician.

How the prices work

Every attribute on every archetype sits in one of four price tiers, and within a tier the price of a point rises with the value you are raising it to. That is why, on a Magician, the same 15 points cost 65 AP of Balance and 177 of Acceleration: both go 75 to 90, one on the cheapest tier and one on the most expensive. Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest attribute to upgrade on a Magician in FC 27?

Balance: 65 AP from 75 to 90, the cheapest route to 90 of any Magician attribute. The Magician's cheapest price tier is Balance, Composure, Volleys and Shot Power.

What is the most expensive upgrade on a Magician?

Sprint Speed: 209 AP from 70 to 90. The Magician's most expensive tier also holds Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing, Short Pass and Agility, and on it every point from 90 up costs 20 AP or more.

How many AP does a Magician get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars are paid from the same budget.

Which Magician specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Magician+ (Acceleration 90, Composure 90, Ball Control 92): 363 AP from a new Magician's starting values. Invader costs 382 AP and Hotshot 436.

Is the Magician or the Spark cheaper for dribbling and shooting?

The Spark for dribbling: Dribbling to 90 costs it 65 AP against the Magician's 177. The Magician for shooting: Shot Power to 90 costs it 76 AP against the Spark's 167.
