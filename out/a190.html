More on FC 27

The rest of what is new

  • The Disruptor — The only brand-new archetype in FC 27 — a ball-winning midfielder built on Roy Keane, with a finished level-40 build.
  • Masteries — A permanent progression layer FC 26 does not have: every archetype you level grants attribute points to every build on your account.
  • What changed for your archetype — One signature PlayStyle instead of four, level-gated perks, and a different specialization structure.
  • Level 40 builds — FC 27 starts you at a 40-level cap with a far tighter budget, so the whole spending order changes.

Frequently asked questions

Which archetype makes the best midfielder in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

On the launch-week meta board Toni Kroos (Maestro · 69.1) and Messi '26 WC (Magician · 73.8) lead the defensive midfielder and attacking midfielder slots. Across the 131 midfielder builds in the catalog the archetypes used are Maestro (54), Recycler (21), Magician (20), Creator (20) and Disruptor (16). Pick the job first; the archetype follows from it.

Are these Pro Clubs builds or Ultimate Team?

Pro Clubs only. Archetypes, PlayStyle+ slots, specializations and the level-40 cap belong to Clubs; Ultimate Team has none of them. Every card opens the build in the Pro Clubs HQ builder, where you can copy it and change any attribute.

How many ability points does a level-40 midfielder have?

Every FC 27 pro reaches level 40 with 962 ability points, one signature PlayStyle+ slot and three regular PlayStyle slots. Each build here spends exactly that budget; copy one and the builder re-prices any change live.

How is the ranking decided?

By how many players have copied each build into their own club, then by views, read from the live database on 22 September 2026. Only house-account builds are listed; members' own builds and copies are left out.
