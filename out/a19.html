Why the Shot Stopper

Unphased when faced with an attacker one-on-one, can be relied upon to make difficult saves. The numbers say the same thing: GK Diving, GK Handling, GK Kicking, GK Positioning and GK Reflexes all cap at 99, and nothing else in the archetype matters as much. The trade is mobility with the ball — Sprint Speed stops at 85 and the outfield technical stats are token — so you are not the keeper who starts attacks. That job belongs to the Sweeper Keeper.

Both archetype perks are about the save after the save. Low Shot Saver — improved ability to save low shots struck close to the body. Ready to Act — faster reactions to rebounds and follow-up shots inside the box. If you want a keeper who plays like an eleventh outfielder instead, compare the two keeper archetypes side by side in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Donnarumma is the wall: GK Reflexes, Positioning and Diving at 99, Handling 98, with Strength 96 and Jumping 96 to rule the box, and the Shot Stopper+ specialization — its Reflex Wall perk boosts Reflexes and Positioning after every save, which is exactly when the second shot arrives.

The Courtois is the Spider: the same elite core but with Acceleration 90 and Agility 90 bought early to meet the specialization, and Sticky Gloves so caught shots stay caught. Pick it if your defence concedes cut-backs and close-range scrambles rather than long-range pot shots.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,120 and 3,137 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Donnarumma, the Courtois.

Specialization order: Shot Stopper+, Spider or Octopus

  • Shot Stopper+ (Jumping 90, GK Handling 92, GK Pos. 90 — 455 AP from the floor): The ultimate Shot Stopper! An impenetrable wall that thrives when peppered with shots. Grants Reflex Wall — after making a save, gain a short boost to gk reflexes and gk positioning.
  • Spider (Acceleration 90, Agility 90, GK Reflexes 92 — 559 AP from the floor): Catches everything thrown at goal, smothering shots that others would parry. Grants Sticky Gloves — caught shots are held more securely, reducing rebound chances.
  • Octopus (Strength 90, GK Diving 92, GK Pos. 90 — 525 AP from the floor): Limbs everywhere. Makes impossible point-blank saves look routine. Grants Rebound Killer — second saves in quick succession receive a boost to gk reflexes.

The honest ranking: Shot Stopper+ for most keepers — Reflex Wall triggers on the rebounds this archetype's perks already feed on. Spider if your games are full of one-on-ones and cut-backs; Octopus if you face point-blank headers from corners every match. All three cost similar AP from the floor, so this is a playstyle call, not a budget one — the full pricing across all 39 specializations is in the specialization planner.

Height and weight

The archetype allows 6'0" to 6'7" and 176–220 lb, and for once, take the inches: reach wins keepers games, and the mobility cost that would sink an outfield build barely registers here. The Donnarumma stands 6'5", the Courtois 6'7" — both at the tall end on purpose.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Shot Stopper archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Shot Stopper is one of the two goalkeeper archetypes, inspired by Oliver Kahn. All five goalkeeping attributes reach 99, and its perks (Low Shot Saver and Ready to Act) are built around making the save and the follow-up save.

What is the best Shot Stopper build?

Drive GK Reflexes, GK Diving and GK Positioning to 99 with GK Handling right behind, then add Strength 96 and Jumping 96 to own the box. The full level-100 build costs 3,120 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Shot Stopper specialization should I take?

Shot Stopper+ for most keepers — its Reflex Wall perk boosts Reflexes and Positioning right after a save, when the rebound arrives. Spider suits one-on-one heavy games, and Octopus is for point-blank chaos in the six-yard box.

How much AP does a full Shot Stopper build cost?

3,120 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Shot Stopper+ criteria alone cost 149 AP on top of the save core.

How tall should a Shot Stopper be?

Tall. The archetype allows up to 6'7", and unlike outfield archetypes there is almost no downside — reach converts directly into saves, which is why the builds in this guide stand 6'5" and 6'7".

Does the Shot Stopper carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Shot Stopper you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
