Shot Stopper questions

What is the best Shot Stopper build in FC 27?

The Shot Stopper builds people copy most are The Octopus, Thibaut Courtois and Joan García: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the list at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Shot Stopper specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Shot stopper+ (Jumping 90, GK Handling 92, GK Pos. 90): 343 AP from a new Shot Stopper's starting values. Octopus costs 391 AP and Spider 553.

What is the Shot Stopper's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Far Reach. The three specializations carry Cross Claimer+ (Shot stopper+), Deflector+ (Octopus) and 1v1 Close Down+ (Spider).

What is cheap to upgrade on a Shot Stopper?

Its cheapest price tier is Acceleration, GK Pos., Reactions and Short Pass; its most expensive is Dribbling, Finishing, Stand Tackle, GK Reflexes, Sprint Speed, GK Kicking, Interceptions and Agility.

How many AP does a Shot Stopper get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.

How tall can a Shot Stopper be in FC 27?

5'10" to 6'6" (179 to 197 cm), at 176 to 220 lbs. The most copied Shot Stopper builds on this page stand 6'0" to 6'6".

Is the Shot Stopper Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled?

A new Shot Stopper starts Lengthy. Height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration decide the type: Explosive needs 182 cm or under with Agility at least 10 above Strength, Lengthy needs 183 cm or over with Strength at least 4 above Agility.
