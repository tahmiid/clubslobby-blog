What's cheap and what's expensive

Each archetype puts each attribute in one of four price tiers. On a Progressor, Composure, Def. Aware, Long Pass and Stand Tackle are the cheapest to raise and Interceptions, Short Pass, Finishing, Sprint Speed, Acceleration, Agility and Dribbling the dearest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Progressor build in FC 27?

The Progressor builds people copy most are Cannavaro '06 WC, Virgil van Dijk and Riccardo Calafiori: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Progressor specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Progressor+ (Long Pass 90, Def. Aware 90, Stand Tackle 92): 211 AP from a new Progressor's starting values. Pioneer costs 516 AP and Janitor 575.

What is the Progressor's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Long Ball Pass.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Progressor?

Its cheapest price tier is Composure, Def. Aware, Long Pass and Stand Tackle; its most expensive is Interceptions, Short Pass, Finishing, Sprint Speed, Acceleration, Agility and Dribbling.

How many AP does a Progressor get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
