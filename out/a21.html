Why the Progressor

A modern centre-back capable of stepping out of the backline to start attacks with progressive passing. The ceilings tell you what kind of centre-back this is: Standing Tackle, Defensive Awareness, Ball Control, Reactions and Jumping all cap at 99 — defending and the first touch, together. What it is not is a bruiser: Strength stops at 93 and Aggression at 90-something short of a Boss, so you win the ball by reading play and stepping in, not by flattening people.

Both perks reward what happens after the ball is won. Restarter — quick restarts after winning the ball launch immediate attacks. Goalkeepers Favourite — improved link-up with your keeper for safe build-up play. If you'd rather dominate duels and clear your lines, that's the Boss — see what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Saliba is the high-line centre-back: Standing Tackle 96, Defensive Awareness 96, and — the point of the build — Acceleration 90 and Sprint Speed 90 through the Janitor specialization, whose Clean Sweeper perk calms your whole defence every time you intercept in your own third. Nobody runs in behind you.

The Cubarsí is the libero: Pioneer takes Dribbling to 92 with Long Pass and Short Pass at 90, and its Deep Architect perk speeds up the long diagonals you hit from your own half. Pick it if your club builds through the back line instead of over it.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,144 and 3,127 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Saliba, the Cubarsí.

Specialization order: Progressor+, Pioneer or Janitor

  • Progressor+ (Long Pass 90, Def. Aware 90, Stand Tackle 92 — 343 AP from the floor): The ultimate Progressor! Dismantles a press with one perfectly weighted pass. Grants Line Splitter — successful line-breaking passes boost the receiver's first touch.
  • Pioneer (Dribbling 92, Long Pass 90, Short Pass 90 — 543 AP from the floor): Strides out of defence, dictating play like a libero from a lost era. Grants Deep Architect — long diagonal switches gain speed and accuracy from your own half.
  • Janitor (Acceleration 90, Sprint Speed 90, Slide Tackle 92 — 545 AP from the floor): Cleans up every loose ball before calmly restarting the play. Grants Clean Sweeper — interceptions in your own third grant a short composure boost to nearby teammates.

The honest ranking: Janitor for a high line — recovery pace on a centre-back changes what your whole club can do tactically. Pioneer if you genuinely dictate from the back. Progressor+ is the balanced default, and its Line Splitter perk boosts the receiver of every line-breaking pass — quietly excellent in a passing club. Frequently asked questions

What is the Progressor archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Progressor is one of the four defender archetypes, inspired by Fernando Hierro. It pairs 99-cap defending (Standing Tackle, Defensive Awareness) with 99-cap Ball Control, and its perks (Restarter and Goalkeepers Favourite) turn won balls into instant attacks.

What is the best Progressor build?

Defence to 96 first — Standing Tackle and Defensive Awareness — then recovery pace at 90 and the passing range (Long Pass 93, Short Pass 93). The full level-100 build costs 3,144 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Progressor or Boss — which centre-back archetype is better?

Boss for pure duels: Strength, Heading and Aggression all reach 99 there. Progressor for a high line and build-up play: it trades the physical ceilings for recovery pace, ball control and progressive passing. Organised clubs usually want one of each.

How much AP does a full Progressor build cost?

3,144 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Janitor criteria alone cost 545 AP from the floor.

Which Progressor specialization should I take?

Janitor if your club defends high — the 90 pace on a centre-back is tactically transformative. Pioneer for a true libero. Progressor+ as the balanced pick whose perk boosts every teammate your line-breaking passes find.

Does the Progressor carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Progressor you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
