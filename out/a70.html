How skill moves work in FC 27

Every pro has a skill-star rating from one to five, and a move is available once your rating meets its tier — the tabs above follow the game's own pages, one per tier, plus juggling tricks. In Clubs, skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other, so the tier you can reach is a build decision, not a fixed cap.

The tiers, at a glance

1 star (7 moves) is available to every build in the game from level one — Directional Nutmeg, Open Up Fake Shot, Flick Up for Volley and FC 27's new Giant Fake Shot. 2 star (12) is the feint tier: body feints, stepovers, drag back, and the new Stop And Go and Drag To Drag. 3 star (12) brings the roulettes, heel chops and feint-and-exit turns, plus the new Foot To Foot and Lateral Heel To Heel.

4 star is the deepest page at 33 moves — ball rolls, fake passes, lane changes, the spins and rainbows, and five new entries including Flair Roulette and Skilled Bridge. 5 star (25) is the showpiece tier: the elastico family, Hocus Pocus, Tornado Spin, the rainbows, and the new First Time Spin, Alternate Elastico Chop and Running Fake Drag. Juggling Tricks (9) round it out — sombrero flicks, around the world and the chest flick, performed while juggling.

13 of these moves are new to FC 27. They wear a badge above, and each has its own guide with when to use it and which builds suit it — start from every new skill move in FC 27. Inputs for moves carried over from FC 26 are unchanged — every move you could already do works identically, so nothing you drilled last year is wasted.
