The FC 26 Isak build

The FC 26 Isak build is a complete forward on the Finisher archetype, and the frame computes as Lengthy. What it does first is written across the top of the sheet: Finishing 99, Shot Power 96, Volleys 95, with Reactions 95 and Heading Acc. 94 underneath.

Its gold PlayStyles are Power Shot, Quick Step, Technical and Gamechanger, which is where the build's real power sits — a gold PlayStyle flares when you press the button it belongs to, and this one is built so those buttons are the ones you press most. It wears the Hunter specialization for the Poacher's Instinct perk: Rebounds and loose balls in the box are reacted to faster. A second specialization is unlocked alongside it, so you can switch the gold between games without spending a point.

Quick answers

What archetype is the Isak build? Finisher, a forward. The Finisher guide covers the AP order behind it and thirteen more finished builds on the same archetype.

How tall is it, and what AcceleRATE does that give? 6'3" at 170 lb, which the builder computes as Lengthy. Change the frame and that can change with it — the AcceleRATE guide has the thresholds.

Which PlayStyles does it run? Gold: Power Shot, Quick Step, Technical and Gamechanger. Regular slots: Finesse Shot, First Touch, Low Driven Shot, Acrobatic and Rapid and more. Every badge on the card above is one of those.

Which specialization? Hunter, with Finisher+ unlocked beside it so you can switch between games.

Can I change it? Yes. Open it, copy it to your account, and every slider is yours — the build is a starting point, not a lock. Most people move the last hundred points toward the role their club actually plays them in.

Is it free? Yes, and there is nothing to install: Pro Clubs HQ runs in the browser.
