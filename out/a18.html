Why the Magician

Combining remarkable control, dribbling, and vision, this player creates chances out of very little, both for themselves and teammates. That is the catalog's own description, and the numbers back it up: Agility, Balance, Ball Control, Curve, Finishing and Reactions all cap at 99 — no other forward archetype puts that six together. The catch is everything else. Standing Tackle stops at 80, Defensive Awareness at 82, Heading Accuracy at 85, and Shot Power at 92: you will not win headers, you will not track back well, and your goals come from placement and curve, not raw power.

Both archetype perks amplify the same job. Getaway Driver — escapes markers with quick direction changes in the final third. Ankle Breaker — successful skill moves briefly slow nearby defenders. If you don't dribble at defenders, pick a different archetype — the full archetype guide covers all 13, and the head-to-head tool will show you exactly what you'd trade against a Spark or a Creator.

The two builds, in full

The Messi is the central 10: Ball Control 97, Dribbling 96, Vision 96, Short Pass 95 and Long Pass 92, with the Invader specialization — its Ghost Runner perk makes runs between the lines harder to track, and it upgrades Incisive Pass to its PlayStyle+ version. You create for the whole team and arrive unmarked in the box.

The Neymar is the flair Magician: Agility 97 and Balance 97 under a 96 touch, Acceleration 95 to get moving, Curve 95 on everything that leaves the boot, and Magician+ boosting the on-ball game. Same touch as the Messi, different geometry — you start wide, invite the fullback, and disappear inside.

These two are the ones this guide takes apart, and the rest of the grid above is built the same way. Pick by where you actually play. Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,107 and 3,016 of 3,167), and opening either in the builder gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Messi, the Neymar.

Specialization order: Magician+, Hotshot or Invader

  • Magician+ (Acceleration 90, Composure 90, Ball Control 92 — 431 AP from the floor): The ultimate Magician! Conjures goals from absolutely nothing. Grants Showtime — flair actions grant a short boost to finishing and curve.
  • Hotshot (Finishing 90, Shot Power 92, Long Shots 90 — 477 AP from the floor): Finds the far corner from impossible angles. Grants Golden Arc — shots from the edge of the box gain curve and accuracy.
  • Invader (Att. Position 90, Vision 90, Long Pass 92 — 419 AP from the floor): Interprets space like no other, arriving unmarked in the box. Grants Ghost Runner — runs between the lines are harder to track, boosting positioning.

The honest ranking: Invader for central players, Magician+ for wide ones, Hotshot only if edge-of-the-box shooting is genuinely your game — it needs Shot Power 92, which is this archetype's exact cap on a stat the build otherwise ignores. Note the overlap: finish the Messi build and Magician+'s criteria are already met too, so the second specialization is a switch, not a second grind. Frequently asked questions

What is the Magician archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Magician is one of the three forward archetypes, inspired by Ronaldinho. It carries the game's best dribbling ceilings — Agility, Balance, Ball Control and Curve all reach 99 — with Finishing at 99 behind them, and its perks (Getaway Driver and Ankle Breaker) reward beating defenders one against one.

What is the best Magician build?

A touch-first build: Ball Control 97, Dribbling 96, Agility 95 and Balance 96, then Finishing 94 and Curve 96 for the end product. The full level-100 build costs 3,107 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Magician specialization should I take?

Invader if you play centrally — its Ghost Runner perk makes runs between the lines harder to track, and it grants Incisive Pass+. Magician+ if you stay wide and on the ball. Hotshot only if your game is shooting from the edge of the box; it is also the most expensive of the three to reach (477 AP from the archetype floor).

How much AP does a full Magician build cost?

3,107 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100, with 60 left over. The three Invader criteria alone cost 419 AP from the floor.

Can a Magician be Explosive?

Yes. Keep the frame short and light and the acceleration high — both builds in this guide (5'7" and 5'9") come out Explosive in the builder's AcceleRATE calculation.

Does the Magician carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Magician you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
