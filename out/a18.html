Frequently asked questions

What is the best Magician build in FC 27?

The Magician builds people copy most are Lamine Yamal, Messi '26 WC and Neymar Jr: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Magician specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Magician+ (Acceleration 90, Composure 90, Ball Control 92): 363 AP from a new Magician's starting values. Invader costs 382 AP and Hotshot 436.

What is the Magician's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

What is cheap to upgrade on a Magician?

Its cheapest price tier is Balance, Composure, Volleys and Shot Power; its most expensive is Stand Tackle, Interceptions, Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing, Short Pass, Agility and Sprint Speed.

How many AP does a Magician get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
