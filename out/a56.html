What it does

Scoop the ball up and turn under it from a standing start.

When to use it

Cornered on the touchline with no forward option. It buys a full rotation in almost no space.

Which builds it suits

Technical forwards who get isolated wide.

Worth knowing

No shoulder button at all, which makes it unusually easy to fire by accident when you meant a fake shot.

4-star requirement

Can your pro do this?

Skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other. Price the jump to 4 stars on your own build before you commit to it.
