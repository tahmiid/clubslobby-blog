What it does

A fake shot with a much larger body swing than the standard one — the full wind-up, then nothing.

When to use it

Standing still with a defender closing. The exaggerated motion sells harder than the normal fake shot, which is why it is a one-star move that still works against people who have seen a thousand fake shots.

Which builds it suits

Everyone. One star means every build in the game can do it from level one, which makes it the single best value move on this list.

Worth knowing

It only fires from a standstill. Coming out of a sprint you get the ordinary fake shot.

1-star requirement

Can your pro do this?

Skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other. Price the jump to 1 stars on your own build before you commit to it.
