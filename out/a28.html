Frequently asked questions

What is the best Spark build in FC 27?

The Spark builds people copy most are Rafael Leão, Corner Specialist and Mohamed Salah: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Spark specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Joker (Att. Position 90, Crossing 92, Long Pass 90): 397 AP from a new Spark's starting values. Ace costs 495 AP and Spark+ 571.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Spark?

Its cheapest price tier is Curve, Dribbling, Vision and Long Shots; its most expensive is Stand Tackle, Acceleration, Agility, Sprint Speed, Interceptions and Finishing.

How many AP does a Spark get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
