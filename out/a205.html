Your build, from a photo of your screen

  • Take a photo of your build in the game, or use a screenshot.
  • Pro Clubs HQ reads it and makes the build for you: archetype, level, attributes, PlayStyles, height and weight.
  • Check it, save it, and it has its own link to share.

How it works

  1. In FC 27, open your pro so the build is on the screen.
  2. Take a photo of the screen with your phone, or a screenshot on the console.
  3. Give it to Pro Clubs HQ. You get the build back, ready to check.
  4. Save it. Share the link, publish it, or take it into the lobby.

Frequently asked questions

When does build from a photo open?

In the next few days. This page will link it the day it does.

What does it read from the photo?

The build: archetype, level, attributes, PlayStyles, height and weight. You check what it read before you save.

Do I need an account?

To save a build, yes: a free Pro Clubs HQ account. Looking at builds needs none.

Is Pro Clubs HQ part of EA?

No. Pro Clubs HQ is an independent site made by Clubs players. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by EA SPORTS.
