Level 40 is the FC 27 cap: 962 AP to spend on the way from the start to the ceiling. Meta board: the best placing on the FC 27 season 1 boards (4-2-3-1), each a top ten.

FC 27 has three defender archetypes: the Boss, the Marauder and the Progressor. The Engine is not in FC 27. Highest ceilings: the Boss for Physical (97), the Marauder for Pace (95), Passing (94) and Scoring (93) and the Progressor for Ball Control (95); Defending is shared at the top. You have 962 AP to spend at level 40, and each archetype prices every attribute on its own four tiers, so the same upgrade costs a different amount on each.

The same prices for all thirteen archetypes: FC 27 AP costs, every archetype compared.

What each one costs to upgrade

Every archetype has four attributes in its cheapest price tier, and they differ:

  • Boss: Aggression, Heading Acc., Jumping and Strength.
  • Marauder: Acceleration, Dribbling, Interceptions and Balance.
  • Progressor: Composure, Def. Aware, Long Pass and Stand Tackle.

The widest gap between the three: 90 Acceleration costs 65 AP on a Marauder and 209 on a Boss or a Progressor, each from its own starting value. Raised to 90, the Boss is the outright cheapest in six of the 29 attributes all three carry, the Marauder in nine and the Progressor in six; in four the lowest price is shared; and in four at most one of them can reach 90.

Boss

The Boss has the group’s highest Physical ceiling and shares the top Defending ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Aggression, Heading Acc., Jumping and Strength; its most expensive tier holds Finishing, Interceptions, Stand Tackle, Acceleration, Sprint Speed, Dribbling, Agility and Short Pass. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the three for Att. Position, Shot Power, Heading Acc., Jumping, Strength and Aggression.

  • Inspired by: Nemanja Vidic. Height: 5'11" to 6'5".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Bruiser. Perks: Shuffler and Box Controller.
  • Stars: 2★ to 3★ skill moves (10 AP), 2★ to 4★ weak foot (55 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Boss+, 244 AP (Strength 90, Aggression 90 and Slide Tackle 92).
  • Recommended roles: Fullback, Defender, Stopper, Centre-Half and Holding.
  • FC 27 meta boards: no. 1 at centre back (71.6).
  • Most copied Boss build: The Wall.

Marauder

The Marauder has the group’s highest Pace, Passing and Scoring ceilings. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Acceleration, Dribbling, Interceptions and Balance; its most expensive tier holds Agility, Stand Tackle, Short Pass and Finishing. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the three for Acceleration, Sprint Speed, Balance, Reactions, Dribbling, Crossing, FK Acc., Interceptions and Stamina.

  • Inspired by: Cafu. Height: 5'6" to 6'2".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Quick Step. Perks: High Speed Crosser and Tackle and Run.
  • Stars: 2★ to 5★ skill moves (65 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (65 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Speedster, 300 AP (Dribbling 90, Sprint Speed 92 and Acceleration 90).
  • Recommended roles: Fullback, Wingback, Falseback, Attacking Wingback, Winger, Wide Playmaker, Wide Midfielder and Wide Half.
  • FC 27 meta boards: no. 1 at fullback (70.9).
  • Most copied Marauder build: The Shadow.

Progressor

The Progressor has the group’s highest Ball Control ceiling and shares the top Defending ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Composure, Def. Aware, Long Pass and Stand Tackle; its most expensive tier holds Interceptions, Short Pass, Finishing, Sprint Speed, Acceleration, Agility and Dribbling. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the three for Composure, Vision, Short Pass, Long Pass, Def. Aware and Stand Tackle.

  • Inspired by: Fernando Hierro. Height: 5'10" to 6'5".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Long Ball Pass. Perks: Restarter and Goalkeepers Favourite.
  • Stars: 2★ to 4★ skill moves (30 AP), 3★ to 4★ weak foot (25 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Progressor+, 211 AP (Long Pass 90, Def. Aware 90 and Stand Tackle 92).
  • Recommended roles: Fullback, Falseback, Defender, Stopper, Ball-Playing Defender, Centre-Half, Holding and Deep-Lying Playmaker.
  • FC 27 meta boards: 9th at centre back (69.2).
  • Most copied Progressor build: Cannavaro '06 WC.
