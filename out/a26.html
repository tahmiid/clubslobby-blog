Maestro questions

What is the best Maestro build in FC 27?

The Maestro builds people copy most are Two-Footed, Iniesta '10 and The Magnet: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the list at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Maestro specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Heartbeat (Agility 92, Stamina 90, Aggression 90): 455 AP from a new Maestro's starting values. Crasher costs 522 AP and Maestro+ 544.

What is the Maestro's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Pinged Pass. The three specializations carry Relentless+ (Heartbeat), First Touch+ (Crasher) and Technical+ (Maestro+).

What is cheap to upgrade on a Maestro?

Its cheapest price tier is Interceptions, Short Pass, Def. Aware and Strength; its most expensive is Agility, Stand Tackle, Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing and Sprint Speed.

How many AP does a Maestro get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.

How tall can a Maestro be in FC 27?

5'4" to 6'2" (162 to 188 cm), at 143 to 187 lbs. The most copied Maestro builds on this page stand 5'7" to 5'11".

Is the Maestro Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled?

A new Maestro starts Controlled. Height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration decide the type: Explosive needs 182 cm or under with Agility at least 10 above Strength, Lengthy needs 183 cm or over with Strength at least 4 above Agility.
