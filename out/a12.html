Frequently asked questions

Which two FC 27 archetypes are most similar?

On attribute ceilings, two pairs tie: the Creator and the Maestro, and the Magician and the Spark, each 1.3 points apart on average across all 29 attributes. For the Creator and the Maestro, the biggest single difference is Interceptions, where the Maestro’s ceiling is 7 points higher. For the Magician and the Spark, the biggest single difference is Crossing, where the Spark’s ceiling is 7 points higher.

Which two archetypes are furthest apart?

The Magician and the Boss: their ceilings are 9.3 points apart on average, and the biggest single differences are 24 points each, in Curve (the Magician higher) and Slide Tackle (the Boss higher).

Which archetype is closest to the Disruptor?

The Marauder, a defender: 2.5 points apart on average, closer than any midfielder. The nearest midfielder is the Recycler, at 2.6.

How different are the two keepers?

The Shot Stopper and the Sweeper Keeper are 3.1 points apart on average across their 34 attributes, and the biggest single difference is Slide Tackle, where the Sweeper Keeper’s ceiling is 20 points higher. All five goalkeeping ceilings are level.

Can I compare a keeper with an outfield archetype?

Yes. Keepers carry all 29 outfield attributes plus five goalkeeping ones, so the tool compares them attribute by attribute; an outfield archetype shows a dash on the goalkeeping rows.
