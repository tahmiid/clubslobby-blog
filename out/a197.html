What's cheap on a Disruptor

Four attributes sit in the Disruptor's cheapest tier, and for three of them no outfield archetype pays less to reach 90: Slide Tackle (75 to 90 for 65 AP), Stamina (75 to 90 for 65 AP) and Crossing (65 to 90 for 82 AP). Sprint Speed costs 76 AP from 70; only the Finisher pays as little.

The cheap tier holds seven more, priced to 90: Att. Position and Reactions (108 AP each from 70); Curve, Jumping, Penalties and Volleys (119 AP each from 65); FK Acc. (136 AP from 50).

What's expensive on a Disruptor

Six attributes are in the Disruptor's most expensive tier. To 90: Interceptions and Short Pass (177 AP each from 75), Stand Tackle (209 AP from 70), Agility (220 AP from 68), Dribbling (234 AP from 65) and Finishing (271 AP from 50).

The ball-winning stats split: Interceptions costs 177 AP from 75 to 90 and Stand Tackle 209 from 70, but Slide Tackle, in the cheapest tier, costs 65 from 75. And past 90 every tier gets steep. On this one, points 91 and 92 cost 20 AP each, 93 and 94 cost 25, then 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 for each point from 95 to 99 — so Short Pass from 75 to its cap of 96 is 332 AP, 35% of your budget for one attribute.

Disruptor vs Magician, Spark, Finisher and Maestro

Each archetype puts each attribute in its own price tier, so the same upgrade can cost more than three times as much on one archetype as on another. Where the others pay less than a Disruptor:

  • Interceptions: 90 costs 65 AP on a Maestro, 177 on a Disruptor.
  • Dribbling: 90 costs 65 AP on a Spark, 234 on a Disruptor.
  • Agility: 90 costs 72 AP on a Finisher, 220 on a Disruptor.

Each specialization asks for three attributes at 90 or 92. Bought from a new Disruptor's starting values, Destroyer is the cheapest at 300 AP (31% of your budget), then Disruptor+ at 454 and Anchor at 618. That is before a single point goes anywhere else.

How the prices work

Every attribute on every archetype sits in one of four price tiers, and within a tier the price of a point rises with the value you are raising it to. That is why, on a Disruptor, the same 15 points cost 65 AP of Stamina and 177 of Interceptions: both go 75 to 90, one on the cheapest tier and one on the most expensive. Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest attribute to upgrade on a Disruptor in FC 27?

Slide Tackle and Stamina: 65 AP each from 75 to 90, the cheapest route to 90 of any Disruptor attribute. The Disruptor's cheapest price tier is Slide Tackle, Stamina, Sprint Speed and Crossing.

What is the most expensive upgrade on a Disruptor?

Finishing: 271 AP from 50 to 90. The Disruptor's most expensive tier also holds Interceptions, Short Pass, Stand Tackle, Agility and Dribbling, and on it every point from 90 up costs 20 AP or more.

How many AP does a Disruptor get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars are paid from the same budget.

Which Disruptor specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Destroyer (Sprint Speed 90, Strength 92, Slide Tackle 90): 300 AP from a new Disruptor's starting values. Disruptor+ costs 454 AP and Anchor 618.

Is the Disruptor or the Maestro cheaper for defending?

It depends on the stat. Interceptions to 90 costs a Maestro 65 AP against the Disruptor's 177, but Slide Tackle to 90 is 65 on a Disruptor and 189 on a Maestro. Stand Tackle is top-tier on both: 209 and 197.
