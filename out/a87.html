Every Pro Clubs build runs one of three acceleration styles — Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled — and the game decides which from exactly four numbers: height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration. The thresholds below are the actual FC 26 rules (the same data the Pro Clubs HQ builder computes from), and the calculator applies them live.

Explosive — the checklist

Short bursts of speed; accelerates fastest over the first few metres.

  • height 182 cm or under (5'0" and below)
  • Agility 65+
  • agility - strength ≥ 10
  • Acceleration 80+

Miss any one line and the build is not Explosive — the conditions are all-or-nothing, checked in order.

Lengthy — the checklist

Slow to start but very hard to catch once at top speed.

  • height 183 cm or over (6'0" and up)
  • Strength 65+
  • strength - agility ≥ 4
  • Acceleration 40+

So yes — you can be Lengthy in Pro Clubs: stand 183 cm or taller, keep Strength at 65+ with Strength beating Agility by 4+, and hold at least 40 Acceleration.

Controlled — the default

Default — any build that meets neither the Explosive nor the Lengthy conditions. There is no Controlled checklist to pass; it is what remains when the other two fail.

Which should you run?

It follows from the body your archetype allows: heights under 183 cm can chase Explosive, heights from 183 cm can chase Lengthy, and the band between them is Controlled territory no matter the stats. Every build card in the app prints its AcceleRATE type, and the archetype tier list notes where each archetype's height range lands.
