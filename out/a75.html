Quick answers

What archetype is the Bolt build? Spark, a midfielder. The Spark guide covers the AP order behind it and thirteen more finished builds on the same archetype.

How tall is it, and what AcceleRATE does that give? 6'2" at 187 lb, which the builder computes as Controlled. Change the frame and that can change with it — the AcceleRATE guide has the thresholds.

Can I change it? Yes. Open it, copy it to your account, and every slider is yours — the build is a starting point, not a lock. Most people move the last hundred points toward the role their club actually plays them in.

Is it free? Yes, and there is nothing to install: Pro Clubs HQ runs in the browser.
