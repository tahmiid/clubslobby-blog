Why the Finisher

Possesses a killer instinct in front of the goal - a beast when one-on-one with the keeper. Ten ceilings at 99 — including Finishing, Att. Position, Composure, Reactions, Volleys and Shot Power — make this statistically the most complete goalscorer in the catalog. What it is not is a target man: Strength and Heading sit below the Target's, and nobody is asking a Finisher to hold the ball up. Get chances; end them.

The perks are one-on-one weapons. Fake to Real — shot fakes create more separation from defenders. 1v1 Master — boost to finishing and composure when one-on-one with the keeper. If your striker game is backing into centre-backs instead of running past them, that's the Target — see what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Isak is the complete striker: Finishing 96, Dribbling 96, Composure 95 and Att. Position 95, with Acceleration and Sprint Speed 94 so the chance-making is self-service — and the Finisher+ specialization, whose Clinical Instinct perk boosts first-time shots inside the box. Cut-back, one touch, goal.

The Salah is the shoulder-runner: Hunter asks for Att. Position 90, Finishing 90 and Curve 92, and Poacher's Instinct reacts to rebounds and loose balls faster. Shorter, Explosive, and always half a step beyond the last defender.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,106 and 3,111 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Isak, the Salah.

Specialization order: Finisher+, Presser or Hunter

  • Finisher+ (Reactions 90, Composure 92, Ball Control 90 — 308 AP from the floor): The ultimate Finisher! One chance is all they ever need. Grants Clinical Instinct — first-time shots inside the box gain a major accuracy boost.
  • Presser (Aggression 90, Stamina 92, Agility 90 — 457 AP from the floor): First line of defence, terrorising backlines into mistakes. Grants Hunt the Ball — pressing defenders forces more errors in their build-up play.
  • Hunter (Att. Position 90, Finishing 90, Curve 92 — 330 AP from the floor): Lives on the shoulder of the last defender, always ready to pounce. Grants Poacher's Instinct — rebounds and loose balls in the box are reacted to faster.

The honest ranking: Finisher+ for almost everyone — first-time shots in the box are the shots a striker actually takes, and its criteria overlap the build's core. Hunter if you play on the last shoulder in a counter-attacking club. Presser is the team-first pick — Stamina 92 and Aggression 90 to be the first line of defence — respectable, but it spends your AP on the other team's build-up. Frequently asked questions

What is the Finisher archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Finisher is one of the three forward archetypes, inspired by Alex Morgan. Ten attributes reach 99 — including Finishing, Att. Position, Composure and Volleys — and its perks (Fake to Real and 1v1 Master) win the one-on-one moments strikers live for.

What is the best Finisher build?

Finishing 96 and Att. Position 95 over Composure 95, then the self-service package — Dribbling 96 with mid-90s pace. The full level-100 build costs 3,106 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Finisher specialization should I take?

Finisher+ for most strikers — Clinical Instinct boosts exactly the first-time box finishes that decide games, and its criteria are stats you buy anyway. Hunter for last-shoulder runners in counter-attacking sides; Presser only if your club presses from the front and you accept the AP cost.

How much AP does a full Finisher build cost?

3,106 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The Finisher+ criteria cost 129 AP beyond the scoring core this build buys anyway.

Finisher or Target — which striker archetype is better?

Finisher for movement, one-on-ones and clean finishing — the higher goal ceiling for a player who runs channels. Target for hold-up play, aerial dominance and physical presence. If your club crosses a lot or plays long, the Target converts more of what it actually receives.

Does the Finisher carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Finisher you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
