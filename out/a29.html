Frequently asked questions

What is the best Finisher build in FC 27?

The Finisher builds people copy most are Mbappé '26 WC, Thierry Henry and Kylian Mbappé: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Finisher specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Presser (Agility 90, Stamina 92, Aggression 90): 332 AP from a new Finisher's starting values. Finisher+ costs 343 AP and Hunter 378.

What is the Finisher's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Low Driven Shot.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Finisher?

Its cheapest price tier is Agility, Dribbling, Sprint Speed and Vision; its most expensive is Stand Tackle, Interceptions, Finishing and Short Pass.

How many AP does a Finisher get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
