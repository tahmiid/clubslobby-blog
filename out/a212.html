EA match data from 99,000 FC 27 league games suggests three things about a Pro Clubs line-up. More humans win more, up to 10: 35.8% with two, 46.6% with five, 57.8% with 10. Your second and third humans are worth most in defence: clubs with two or more human defenders win +3.6 points more often than clubs of the same size without them. And the keeper is the last place to put one.

Where to put your humans

Start at the back. Clubs with two or more humans in defence win +3.6 points more often than clubs with the same number of humans who play them elsewhere.

The keeper is the exception. Only 12.6% of clubs play a human in goal, and in EA match data those clubs win 2.3 points less often than clubs of the same size without one. Your next human goes in defence before in goal.

So a club of three plays a centre-back, a holding midfielder and a striker. A club of five adds the second centre-back and a creator. Once the back line is human, the rest is taste. Running the club too? The club owner's guide covers recruiting and club modes.

How many humans you need

Every extra human helps until about 10: the win rate climbs from 35.8% with two to 57.8% with 10. The table at the top shows where it comes from. Goals scored barely move, from 2.3 to 2.3 a game, but goals conceded fall from 2.8 to 1.3. More humans do not make a club more dangerous; they make it harder to score against.

The most common shape at the top of the game. Two defensive midfielders cover the back four, the CAM links the lines, and the two wide forwards play narrow, close to the striker.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best formation for Pro Clubs in FC 27?

4-2-1-3 is the most common choice at the top of the game: two CDMs protect the back four and the front three play narrow. For a club with few humans, EA match data suggests the bigger question is where they play: two or more human defenders are worth +3.6 points of win rate.

Should we have a human goalkeeper in Pro Clubs?

Not before your defence is covered. Only 12.6% of clubs in EA match data play a human keeper, and those clubs win 2.3 points less often than clubs of the same size without one. Put your next human at centre-back or holding midfield first.

How many players do you need for Pro Clubs?

You can play with two, but clubs win more as they add humans: 35.8% with two, 46.6% with five and 57.8% with 10. Most of the gain is at the back: clubs with more humans concede far fewer goals (2.8 a game with two, 1.2 with eleven) while scoring about the same.

Which archetypes work best together?

In EA match data the clearest pair is Progressor with Progressor (+4.6 points), then Finisher with Recycler and Marauder with Progressor.

How many formations are there in FC 27 Clubs?

29. Lists of 45 count Ultimate Team's numbered names (4-3-3 (2), (3), (4)) as separate formations; Clubs names the same shapes Attack, Defend, Holding, Narrow, Wide and Flat.
