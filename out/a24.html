Why the Marauder

A defensive specialist whose pulsating pace means they're also comfortable and effective going forward. The three 99 ceilings say it plainly: Crossing, Dribbling, Reactions. This is the defender that attacks. The trade is in the middle — Heading Accuracy stops at 90 and the pure centre-back stats sit below both the Boss and the Progressor — so a Marauder lives on the touchline, not in the six-yard box.

The perks are built for the overlap. High Speed Crosser — deliver accurate crosses while sprinting at full speed. Tackle and Run — win the ball and immediately burst forward with a speed boost. If endless pressing matters more to you than raw pace, that's the Engine — see what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Alexander-Arnold is the playmaker at fullback: Crossing 99, Curve 96, Long Pass, Short Pass and Vision all 92, with Dead Ball, Incisive Pass and Long Ball Pass equipped — plus the Marauder+ specialization, whose Overlap Threat perk boosts crossing accuracy in the final third. Set pieces, switches, cut-backs: every assist type from one build.

The Nuno Mendes is the sprint build: Speedster stacks Acceleration 90, Sprint Speed 92 and Dribbling 90, and Jet Fuel boosts Dribbling and Balance the moment you hit top speed. Wingers simply stop receiving the ball on your side.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,110 and 3,112 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Alexander-Arnold, the Nuno Mendes.

Specialization order: Marauder+, Speedster or Athlete

  • Marauder+ (Sprint Speed 92, Aggression 90, Slide Tackle 90 — 343 AP from the floor): The ultimate Marauder! Owns the entire flank from box to box. Grants Overlap Threat — overlapping runs grant a crossing accuracy boost in the final third.
  • Speedster (Acceleration 90, Sprint Speed 92, Dribbling 90 — 545 AP from the floor): Pure pace. Leaves wingers trailing and recovers before they can blink. Grants Jet Fuel — reaching top speed grants a short boost to dribbling and balance.
  • Athlete (Strength 92, Aggression 90, Def. Aware 90 — 452 AP from the floor): An endurance machine, repeating sprints all match without fading. Grants Iron Lungs — sprint recovery is drastically improved during long attacking spells.

The honest ranking: Marauder+ if you attack more than you defend — the overlap perk pays out on the thing you do most. Speedster if your flank keeps getting run at; it is the best defensive insurance pace can buy. Athlete suits box-to-box wing-backs in long-possession clubs — Iron Lungs keeps the sprints coming — but it buys Strength 92 on the game's lightest defender. Frequently asked questions

What is the Marauder archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Marauder is one of the four defender archetypes, inspired by Cafu. Crossing, Dribbling and Reactions all reach 99 on the fastest defender frame in the game, and its perks (High Speed Crosser and Tackle and Run) are built for the overlap.

What is the best Marauder build?

Crossing to its 99 cap with Curve 96 behind it, Sprint Speed 92, and a full passing set at 92 — a playmaker who defends the touchline. The full level-100 build costs 3,110 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Marauder specialization should I take?

Marauder+ for attacking fullbacks — its Overlap Threat perk boosts the final-third crossing this build maxes anyway. Speedster if you face fast wingers every match. Athlete for box-to-box endurance at the cost of buying Strength on a light frame.

How much AP does a full Marauder build cost?

3,110 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Marauder+ criteria alone cost 214 AP from the floor.

Is the Marauder good at centre-back?

No — and it isn’t trying to be. Heading caps at 90 and the aerial-physical package belongs to the Boss. The Marauder is a fullback and wing-back specialist: pace, delivery, and enough tackling to hold the wide channel.

Does the Marauder carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Marauder you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
