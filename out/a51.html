What it does

Kill the ball dead, then push off again in the same motion.

When to use it

Running at a retreating defender. The stop buys the half-yard their momentum costs them; the go takes it.

Which builds it suits

Anything with Agility and Acceleration to punish the gap — but at two stars, most builds qualify.

2-star requirement

Can your pro do this?

Skill stars are an attribute you buy with AP like any other. Price the jump to 2 stars on your own build before you commit to it.
