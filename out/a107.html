Explosive: the checklist

Short bursts of speed; accelerates fastest over the first few metres. Every line has to pass:

  • Height 182 cm or less (5'11" and under)
  • Agility 65+
  • Agility at least 10 higher than Strength
  • Acceleration 80+

The cheapest archetypes to make Explosive, from a new pro’s starting values: the Marauder, 15 AP (Acceleration 80), the Spark, 42 AP (Acceleration 80) and the Finisher, 53 AP (Agility 80, Acceleration 80). The Sweeper Keeper cannot be Explosive: it starts at 6'0".

Lengthy: the checklist

Slow to start but very hard to catch once at top speed. Every line has to pass:

  • Height 183 cm or more (6'1" and up)
  • Strength 65+
  • Strength at least 4 higher than Agility
  • Acceleration 40+

Can you be Lengthy in Pro Clubs? Yes, on every archetype. Six are Lengthy with no upgrades once they are 6'1" or taller: Target, Disruptor, Recycler, Boss, Shot Stopper and Sweeper Keeper. The dearest to convert is the Spark, 62 AP for Strength 79, out of the 962 you have at level 40.

Controlled: the default

Controlled is any build that meets neither the Explosive nor the Lengthy conditions: there is no checklist to pass. Nine of the 13 archetypes start Controlled, and a 6'0" pro (182.9 cm, between the two height cut-offs) always is.

Why the calculator can show two answers

The menu reads the attributes you set. In a match, height and weight shift Acceleration, Agility and Strength (on a keeper, Acceleration and Strength) for free, measured from the middle of your archetype’s range, and the type is read again. How much depends on the distance: 1–4 cm is 1, 5–8 cm is 2, 9–12 cm is 3, 13–16 cm is 4 points per attribute for height; 1–8 kg is 1, 9–16 kg is 2 for weight. When the two readings differ the calculator shows both, the way the builder prints “Controlled (in-game Explosive)”. What a taller or heavier body shifts, attribute by attribute, is in AcceleRATE explained.

Frequently asked questions

Is 6'0" Lengthy in FC 27?

No. 6'0" is 182.9 cm, just under Lengthy’s 183 cm, and it is over Explosive’s 182 cm too, so a 6'0" pro is Controlled whatever its attributes. Lengthy starts at 6'1".

How tall do you have to be to be Lengthy in Pro Clubs?

183 cm or taller, which in whole inches is 6'1" and up. You also need Strength 65+, Strength at least 4 above Agility, and Acceleration 40+.

What is the tallest you can be and still be Explosive?

182 cm, which in whole inches is 5'11" (180.3 cm). Explosive also needs Agility 65+, Agility at least 10 above Strength, and Acceleration 80+.

Can every archetype be Lengthy?

Yes, all 13. Six are Lengthy at 6'1" or taller with no upgrades (Target, Disruptor, Recycler, Boss, Shot Stopper and Sweeper Keeper); the dearest to convert is the Spark, 62 AP for Strength 79.

Does weight change your AcceleRATE?

Not the type the menu shows. In a match, weight shifts Acceleration, Agility and Strength (on a keeper, Acceleration and Strength) for free, measured from the middle of your archetype’s weight range, and the type is read again from the shifted values; so can height. The calculator shows both readings.
