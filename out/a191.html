Frequently asked questions

Which archetype makes the best defender in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

On the launch-week meta board Vidić '08 CL (Boss · 70.8) and Roberto Carlos '02 WC (Marauder · 68.3) lead the centre-back and full-back slots. Across the 141 defender builds in the catalog the archetypes used are Marauder (59), Boss (54) and Progressor (28). Pick the job first; the archetype follows from it.

Are these Pro Clubs builds or Ultimate Team?

Pro Clubs only. Archetypes, PlayStyle+ slots, specializations and the level-40 cap belong to Clubs; Ultimate Team has none of them. Every card opens the build in the Pro Clubs HQ builder, where you can copy it and change any attribute.

How many ability points does a level-40 defender have?

Every FC 27 pro reaches level 40 with 962 ability points, one signature PlayStyle+ slot and three regular PlayStyle slots. Each build here spends exactly that budget; copy one and the builder re-prices any change live.

How is the ranking decided?

By how many players have copied each build into their own club, then by views, read from the live database on 22 September 2026. Only house-account builds are listed; members' own builds and copies are left out.
