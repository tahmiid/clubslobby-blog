Level 40 is the FC 27 cap: 962 AP to spend on the way from the start to the ceiling. Meta board: the best placing on the FC 27 season 1 boards (4-2-3-1), each a top ten.

FC 27 has four forward archetypes: the Finisher, the Magician, the Spark and the Target. Highest ceilings: the Finisher for Scoring (99), the Spark for Pace (97) and the Target for Physical (95); Ball Control, Passing and Defending are shared at the top. You have 962 AP to spend at level 40, and each archetype prices every attribute on its own four tiers, so the same upgrade costs a different amount on each.

Every upgrade priced, one archetype per page: Finisher · Magician · Spark · all 13 compared.

What each one costs to upgrade

Every archetype has four attributes in its cheapest price tier, and they differ:

  • Finisher: Agility, Dribbling, Sprint Speed and Vision.
  • Magician: Balance, Composure, Volleys and Shot Power.
  • Spark: Curve, Dribbling, Vision and Long Shots.
  • Target: Short Pass, Acceleration, Ball Control and Curve.

The widest gap between the four: 90 Dribbling costs 65 AP on a Spark and 209 on a Target, each from its own starting value. Raised to 90, the Finisher is the outright cheapest in two of the 29 attributes all four carry, the Magician in four, the Spark in six and the Target in six; in seven the lowest price is shared; and in four at most one of them can reach 90.

Finisher

The Finisher has the group’s highest Scoring ceiling and shares the top Ball Control and Defending ceilings. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Agility, Dribbling, Sprint Speed and Vision; its most expensive tier holds Stand Tackle, Interceptions, Finishing and Short Pass. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Sprint Speed and Agility.

  • Inspired by: Alex Morgan. Height: 5'5" to 6'3".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Low Driven Shot. Perks: Fake to Real and 1v1 Master.
  • Stars: 3★ to 5★ skill moves (40 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (85 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Presser, 332 AP (Agility 90, Stamina 92 and Aggression 90).
  • Recommended roles: Playmaker, Inside Forward, Shadow Striker, Target Forward, False 9, Poacher, Advanced Forward and Classic 10.
  • FC 27 meta boards: no. 1 at striker (73.7).
  • Most copied Finisher build: Mbappé '26 WC.

Magician

The Magician shares the top Ball Control and Passing ceilings. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Balance, Composure, Volleys and Shot Power; its most expensive tier holds Stand Tackle, Interceptions, Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing, Short Pass, Agility and Sprint Speed. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Balance, Composure, Shot Power and Volleys.
