Why the Target

Classic hold-up player that harnesses their physicality in duels both on the ground and in the air. The ceilings read like a heavyweight's card: Strength, Shot Power, Heading Accuracy, Composure, Reactions, FK Accuracy and Penalties all at 99. It is the striker defenders can't move — and the trade is burst: Acceleration caps at 90, so a Target wins position, not footraces. Every cross, long ball and second ball in the box belongs to you.

The perks are back-to-goal weapons. 6th Sense — shoot with back to goal in the attacking third to gain a short boost to finishing, balance, and composure. Physical Shooter — shield off an opponent, then shoot in the box for a boost to strength, shot power, and balance. If you'd rather run past centre-backs than through them, that's the Finisher — see what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Kane is the complete nine: Shot Power, Finishing and Att. Position at 96, Heading 93 — and then the part that makes it special: the Roamer specialization, with Vision 92, Short Pass 93 and Long Pass 91, whose Blind Passer perk boosts back-to-goal passes in the final third. Drop deep, release the runner, arrive for the return.

The Gyökeres is the freight train: Runner pairs Sprint Speed 92 with Strength 90 and Att. Position 90, and High Speed Shooter boosts shots taken at a full sprint. No drop-deep subtlety — just the channel, the shoulder, and the far corner.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,064 and 3,107 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Kane, the Gyökeres.

Specialization order: Target+, Roamer or Runner

  • Target+ (Agility 90, Jumping 92, Volleys 90 — 392 AP from the floor): The ultimate Target! Unplayable in the air and impossible to out-muscle. Grants Line Breaker — receive a line-breaking pass in the attacking third for a boost to dribbling, balance, and composure.
  • Roamer (Vision 90, Long Pass 90, Short Pass 92 — 573 AP from the floor): Drops deep to link play, pulling defenders out of position. Grants Blind Passer — pass with back to goal in attacking third to gain a short boost to finishing, balance, and composure.
  • Runner (Sprint Speed 92, Strength 90, Att. Position 90 — 474 AP from the floor): Runs the channels relentlessly, stretching backlines to breaking point. Grants High Speed Shooter — shoot while at full sprint for a boost to shot power, strength, and aggression.

The honest ranking: Roamer for organised clubs — a nine who links play makes everyone else better, and Kane has been the proof of concept for a decade. Runner for counter-attacking sides with space to run into. Target+ is the pure fortress — Jumping 92 and Volleys 90 for the box presence — ideal if your club's whole plan is crosses. Frequently asked questions

What is the Target archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Target is one of the three forward archetypes, inspired by Zlatan Ibrahimović. Strength, Shot Power, Heading Accuracy, Composure and Reactions all reach 99, and its perks (6th Sense and Physical Shooter) trigger with your back to goal — the hold-up game, rewarded.

What is the best Target build?

Finishing, Shot Power and Att. Position at 96 with Heading 93 and the Roamer passing set (Vision 92, Short Pass 93) so you link play as well as end it. The full level-100 build costs 3,064 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Target specialization should I take?

Roamer to be the complete nine — its Blind Passer perk turns hold-up play into assists. Runner for counter-attacking clubs with grass to attack. Target+ if your club crosses relentlessly and you want the air locked down.

How much AP does a full Target build cost?

3,064 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Roamer criteria alone cost 573 AP from the floor.

Should a Target be Lengthy?

Yes. The frame that wins headers makes Lengthy nearly automatic, and it fits how a Target actually runs — sustained chases into channels rather than standing-start bursts. Explosive Targets exist, but they spend height the archetype wants to keep.

Does the Target carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Target you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
