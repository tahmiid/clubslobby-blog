Frequently asked questions

What is the best Target build in FC 27?

The Target builds people copy most are CL Ronaldo, Erling Haaland and Zlatan the Bicycle: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Target specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Target+ (Agility 90, Jumping 92, Volleys 90): 425 AP from a new Target's starting values. Roamer costs 437 AP and Runner 474.

What is the Target's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Precision Header.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Target?

Its cheapest price tier is Short Pass, Acceleration, Ball Control and Curve; its most expensive is Stand Tackle, Interceptions, Agility, Dribbling and Sprint Speed.

How many AP does a Target get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
