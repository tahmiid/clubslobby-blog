Why the Boss

Wins the ball with imposing physicality. Willing to put everything on the line for the team. Seven attributes cap at 99 — Strength, Aggression, Jumping, Heading Accuracy, Standing Tackle, Slide Tackle and Reactions — which is every stat a duel can be decided by. The trade is on the ball: this archetype starts Lengthy by default, and while its passing ceilings are respectable, nobody picks a Boss to dictate play. You pick it so nothing gets past.

The perks double down. Shuffler — improved lateral movement when jockeying and containing attackers. Box Controller — dominates the penalty area, boosting clearances and aerial duels. If you'd rather step out and build play, the Progressor is the other centre-back — see exactly what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Van Dijk is the complete centre-back: Strength 96, Standing Tackle 96 and Defensive Awareness 96, but the signature is the Capitano specialization — Agility 90 and Reactions 90 on a giant, plus Last Line Hero, which boosts every nearby defender's awareness when you block or clear inside your own box.

The Bastoni is the pure enforcer: Boss+ pushes Slide Tackle 92, Aggression 90 and Strength 90, and Immovable Object grants a Strength and Balance boost each time you win a physical duel — which, with these numbers, is every time.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,145 and 3,167 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Van Dijk, the Bastoni.

Specialization order: Boss+, Enforcer or Capitano

  • Boss+ (Slide Tackle 92, Aggression 90, Strength 90 — 271 AP from the floor): The ultimate Boss! Throws their body in front of everything that moves. Grants Immovable Object — winning a physical duel grants a short boost to strength and balance.
  • Enforcer (Composure 92, Vision 90, Ball Control 90 — 548 AP from the floor): Sets the tone with crunching challenges that opponents remember. Grants Midfield General — hard challenges in midfield briefly reduce the opponent's composure.
  • Capitano (Agility 90, Reactions 90, Def. Aware 92 — 519 AP from the floor): Reads danger early and leads the backline with towering interventions. Grants Last Line Hero — blocks and clearances inside your box boost nearby defenders' awareness.

The honest ranking: Capitano is the best all-round pick — agility and reactions are the Boss's natural blind spot, and its perk strengthens the whole box, not just you. Boss+ if you simply want the duel numbers maximal. Enforcer is the surprise: Composure 92, Vision 90 and Ball Control 90 turn a Boss into a passable deep midfielder, but you're paying for stats outside the archetype's identity. Frequently asked questions

What is the Boss archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Boss is one of the four defender archetypes, inspired by Nemanja Vidić. Seven duel stats reach 99 — Strength, Aggression, Jumping, Heading Accuracy, both tackles and Reactions — and its perks (Shuffler and Box Controller) rule the penalty area.

What is the best Boss build?

Strength, Standing Tackle and Defensive Awareness to 96 with Jumping 94 and Heading 92 behind them, plus Agility 90 via Capitano so quick forwards can't just turn you. The full level-100 build costs 3,145 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Boss specialization should I take?

Capitano for most players — Agility 90 and Reactions 90 fix the one thing attackers try to exploit on a Boss, and Last Line Hero boosts every defender near you. Boss+ if you want maximum duel numbers; Enforcer only if you moonlight in midfield.

How much AP does a full Boss build cost?

3,145 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Capitano criteria alone cost 360 AP from the floor.

Should a Boss be Lengthy?

Yes — embrace it. The archetype defaults Lengthy, tall frames protect the aerial ceilings, and Lengthy acceleration wins the 40-yard recovery chases a centre-back actually runs. Explosive is for the people you are marking.

Does the Boss carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Boss you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
