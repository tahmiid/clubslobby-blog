Boss questions

What is the best Boss build in FC 27?

The Boss builds people copy most are The Wall, Vidić '08 CL and Federico Valverde: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the list at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Boss specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Boss+ (Strength 90, Aggression 90, Slide Tackle 92): 244 AP from a new Boss's starting values. Capitano costs 519 AP and Enforcer 548.

What is the Boss's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Bruiser. The three specializations carry Slide Tackle+ (Boss+), Block+ (Capitano) and Press Proven+ (Enforcer).

What is cheap to upgrade on a Boss?

Its cheapest price tier is Aggression, Heading Acc., Jumping and Strength; its most expensive is Finishing, Interceptions, Stand Tackle, Acceleration, Sprint Speed, Dribbling, Agility and Short Pass.

How many AP does a Boss get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.

How tall can a Boss be in FC 27?

5'11" to 6'5" (180 to 196 cm), at 176 to 220 lbs. The most copied Boss builds on this page stand 5'11" to 6'4".

Is the Boss Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled?

A new Boss starts Lengthy. Height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration decide the type: Explosive needs 182 cm or under with Agility at least 10 above Strength, Lengthy needs 183 cm or over with Strength at least 4 above Agility.
