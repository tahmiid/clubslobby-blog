How the ranking works, and what it does not say

The meta engine scores builds, not reputations: each season declares a formation and imports a reference build per position, and every published build is measured against it. This season’s board leaders score 79.6 to 93.5; a perfect 100 is structurally unreachable. Two things this list deliberately is not: it is not a verdict on an archetype’s design (a B tier archetype can be the right pick for your club’s system), and it is not frozen. Boards move with every published build, and the live board is always ahead of this page. Players also vote there on the next season’s formation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best archetype in FC 27?

By the FC 27 Season 1 boards, the Finisher: its 93.5 at striker is the highest score any FC 27 build holds. But "best" is per position: Goalkeeper: Shot Stopper, Centre back: Boss, Fullback: Marauder, Defensive mid: Disruptor, Attacking mid: Creator, Wide mid: Magician, Winger: Magician, Striker: Finisher.

How is this tier list ranked?

It is not our opinion: placements follow the Pro Clubs HQ meta engine, which scores every published FC 27 build 0 to 100 against the Season 1 reference XI (4-2-1-3). S tier tops a position board, A tier makes a top four, B tier is outside every top four this season.

Which archetypes are in B tier?

The Sweeper Keeper, the Recycler, the Progressor, the Maestro and the Target are in B tier: the Sweeper Keeper is 5th at goalkeeper; the Recycler is 5th at defensive mid; the Progressor is 10th at centre back; the Maestro is not in any board’s top ten; the Target is not in any board’s top ten. B tier is a verdict on this season’s boards, not on the archetype.

Why does no build score 100?

The formula’s perfect 100 is structurally out of reach, because no single build can max every component it measures. This season the eight boards are led at scores from 79.6 to 93.5.

Will the tier list change?

Yes, twice over: boards move as new builds publish, and each meta season sets a new formation and reference XI. Players vote on what the next season should be on the live Meta page (19 players picked Season 1).
