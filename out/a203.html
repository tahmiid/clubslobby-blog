What height and weight change

A body away from the archetype's starting one moves a few attributes for free, up and down, and the AcceleRATE type has a height line: 182 cm or shorter for Explosive, 183 cm or taller for Lengthy. The rest of the AcceleRATE rules are in AcceleRATE explained, and the calculator checks your own numbers.

Does height change AcceleRATE in FC 27?

Yes. Explosive needs a pro of 182 cm or shorter and Lengthy one of 183 cm or taller, on top of the Agility, Strength and Acceleration each type asks for. Go by centimetres: the game shows several centimetre heights under the same feet-and-inches label.

Does weight change your attributes in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

Yes. For an outfield pro, every step heavier than the archetype's starting weight raises Balance, Jumping and Strength and lowers Acceleration, Agility and Sprint Speed; lighter does the reverse. Height works the same way: taller raises Jumping, Sprint Speed and Strength and lowers Acceleration, Agility and Balance.

Where do these numbers come from?

The ranges are the game's own, per archetype. The most-built heights and weights are counted from 399 builds players published on Pro Clubs HQ (their own builds; copies are not counted), read on 29 September 2026.
