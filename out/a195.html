What's cheap on a Finisher

Four attributes sit in the Finisher's cheapest tier. Agility costs 72 AP from 72; only the Creator pays as little. Dribbling costs 76 AP from 70; only the Spark pays less. Sprint Speed costs 76 AP from 70; only the Disruptor pays as little. Vision costs 82 AP from 65; only the Spark pays less.

The cheap tier holds ten more, priced to 90: Att. Position (71 AP from 80); Ball Control, Penalties, Reactions and Volleys (92 AP each from 75); Curve (108 AP from 70); Aggression, FK Acc., Jumping and Stamina (119 AP each from 65).

What's expensive on a Finisher

Four attributes are in the Finisher's most expensive tier. Two are defensive (Stand Tackle and Interceptions); the other two are Finishing (177 AP from 75) and Short Pass (209 AP from 70).

The catch is in the name: Finishing costs a Finisher 177 AP from 75 to 90, exactly what a Magician pays for it. And past 90 every tier gets steep. On this one, points 91 and 92 cost 20 AP each, 93 and 94 cost 25, then 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 for each point from 95 to 99 — so Finishing from 75 to its cap of 99 is 467 AP, 49% of your budget for one attribute.

Finisher vs Magician, Spark, Maestro and Disruptor

Each archetype puts each attribute in its own price tier, so the same upgrade can cost more than three times as much on one archetype as on another. Where the others pay less than a Finisher:

  • Short Pass: 90 costs 65 AP on a Maestro, 209 on a Finisher.
  • Long Shots: 90 costs 82 AP on a Spark, 179 on a Finisher.
  • Composure: 90 costs 72 AP on a Magician, 129 on a Finisher.

Each specialization asks for three attributes at 90 or 92. Bought from a new Finisher's starting values, Presser is the cheapest at 332 AP (35% of your budget), then Finisher+ at 343 and Hunter at 378. That is before a single point goes anywhere else.

How the prices work

Every attribute on every archetype sits in one of four price tiers, and within a tier the price of a point rises with the value you are raising it to. That is why, on a Finisher, the same 20 points cost 76 AP of Sprint Speed and 209 of Short Pass: both go 70 to 90, one on the cheapest tier and one on the most expensive. Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest attribute to upgrade on a Finisher in FC 27?

Att. Position: 71 AP from 80 to 90, the cheapest route to 90 of any Finisher attribute (it starts high). The Finisher's cheapest price tier is Agility, Dribbling, Sprint Speed and Vision.

What is the most expensive upgrade on a Finisher?

Short Pass: 209 AP from 70 to 90. The Finisher's most expensive tier also holds Finishing, and on it every point from 90 up costs 20 AP or more.

How many AP does a Finisher get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars are paid from the same budget.

Which Finisher specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Presser (Agility 90, Stamina 92, Aggression 90): 332 AP from a new Finisher's starting values. Finisher+ costs 343 AP and Hunter 378.

Which archetype gets Finishing to 90 for the least AP?

The Target, at 143 AP from its starting 72. Of the five archetypes on these pages, the Finisher and the Magician pay 177, the Spark and the Maestro 234, and the Disruptor 271.
