Level 40 is the FC 27 cap: 962 AP to spend on the way from the start to the ceiling. Meta board: the best placing on the FC 27 season 1 boards (4-2-3-1), each a top ten.

FC 27 has four midfielder archetypes: the Creator, the Disruptor, the Maestro and the Recycler. The Disruptor is the new one in FC 27. Highest ceilings: the Creator for Passing (98) and Scoring (98), the Disruptor for Physical (96), the Maestro for Ball Control (98) and the Recycler for Defending (95); Pace is shared at the top. You have 962 AP to spend at level 40, and each archetype prices every attribute on its own four tiers, so the same upgrade costs a different amount on each.

Every upgrade priced, one archetype per page: Disruptor · Maestro · all 13 compared.

What each one costs to upgrade

Every archetype has four attributes in its cheapest price tier, and they differ:

  • Creator: Ball Control, Long Pass, Reactions and Agility.
  • Disruptor: Slide Tackle, Stamina, Sprint Speed and Crossing.
  • Maestro: Interceptions, Short Pass, Def. Aware and Strength.
  • Recycler: Aggression, Stand Tackle, Stamina and Acceleration.

The widest gap between the four: 90 Stand Tackle costs 65 AP on a Recycler and 234 on a Creator, each from its own starting value. Raised to 90, the Creator is the outright cheapest in ten of the 29 attributes all four carry, the Disruptor in five, the Maestro in five and the Recycler in four; in five the lowest price is shared.

Creator

The Creator has the group’s highest Passing and Scoring ceilings and shares the top Pace ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Ball Control, Long Pass, Reactions and Agility; its most expensive tier holds Dribbling, Short Pass, Interceptions, Acceleration, Finishing, Sprint Speed and Stand Tackle. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Agility, Reactions, Ball Control, Dribbling, Composure, Vision, Long Pass, Curve, Volleys and Penalties.

  • Inspired by: Andres Iniesta. Height: 5'4" to 6'2".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Incisive Pass. Perks: Grasshopper Passer and Bullseye Passer.
  • Stars: 2★ to 5★ skill moves (65 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (65 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Creator+, 308 AP (Vision 92, Crossing 90 and Long Pass 90).
  • Recommended roles: Box-to-Box, Playmaker, Half-Winger, Wide Playmaker, Wide Midfielder, Shadow Striker and Classic 10.
  • FC 27 meta boards: not in any board’s top ten.
  • Most copied Creator build: Zinedine Zidane.

Disruptor

The Disruptor has the group’s highest Physical ceiling and shares the top Pace ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Slide Tackle, Stamina, Sprint Speed and Crossing; its most expensive tier holds Interceptions, Short Pass, Stand Tackle, Agility, Dribbling and Finishing. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Sprint Speed, Crossing, Heading Acc., Slide Tackle and Stamina.

  • Inspired by: Roy Keane. Height: 5'6" to 6'2".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Jockey. Perks: Tracker and Presser.
  • Stars: 2★ to 4★ skill moves (30 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (85 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Destroyer, 300 AP (Sprint Speed 90, Strength 92 and Slide Tackle 90).
  • Recommended roles: Fullback, Falseback, Box-to-Box, Wide Midfielder, Wingback, Holding and Winger.
  • FC 27 meta boards: 8th at fullback (68.8).
  • Most copied Disruptor build: Gennaro Gattuso.

Maestro

The Maestro has the group’s highest Ball Control ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Interceptions, Short Pass, Def. Aware and Strength; its most expensive tier holds Agility, Stand Tackle, Acceleration, Dribbling, Finishing and Sprint Speed. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Short Pass, Long Shots, Interceptions, Def. Aware and Strength.

  • Inspired by: Toni Kroos. Height: 5'4" to 6'2".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Pinged Pass. Perks: Fly Trap and Eagle Eyes.
  • Stars: 2★ to 5★ skill moves (65 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (65 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Heartbeat, 455 AP (Agility 92, Stamina 90 and Aggression 90).
  • Recommended roles: Holding, Deep-Lying Playmaker, Box-to-Box, Playmaker and Wide Playmaker.
  • FC 27 meta boards: no. 1 at defensive mid (69.2).
  • Most copied Maestro build: Vitinha.

Recycler

The Recycler has the group’s highest Defending ceiling. Its four cheapest attributes to raise are Aggression, Stand Tackle, Stamina and Acceleration; its most expensive tier holds Interceptions, Sprint Speed, Agility, Dribbling and Finishing. Raised to 90, it is the outright cheapest of the four for Acceleration, Shot Power, Stand Tackle and Aggression.

  • Inspired by: Claude Makelele. Height: 5'8" to 6'4".
  • Signature PlayStyle: Intercept. Perks: Press and Pass and Physical Passer.
  • Stars: 2★ to 4★ skill moves (23 AP), 3★ to 5★ weak foot (65 AP).
  • Cheapest specialization: Thief, 387 AP (Interceptions 90, Def. Aware 90 and Stand Tackle 92).
  • Recommended roles: Stopper, Ball-Playing Defender, Centre-Half, Holding, Deep-Lying Playmaker, Box-to-Box, Playmaker and Wide Half.
  • FC 27 meta boards: 10th at defensive mid (67.8).
  • Most copied Recycler build: Rodri '26 WC.

More on FC 27

The rest of what is new

  • The Disruptor — The only brand-new archetype in FC 27 — a ball-winning midfielder built on Roy Keane, with a finished level-40 build.
  • Masteries — A permanent progression layer FC 26 does not have: every archetype you level grants attribute points to every build on your account.
  • What changed for your archetype — One signature PlayStyle instead of four, level-gated perks, and a different specialization structure.
  • Level 40 builds — FC 27 starts you at a 40-level cap with a far tighter budget, so the whole spending order changes.
