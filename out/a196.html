What's cheap on a Maestro

Four attributes sit in the Maestro's cheapest tier, and for one of them no outfield archetype pays less to reach 90: Interceptions (75 to 90 for 65 AP). Short Pass costs 65 AP from 75; only the Target pays as little. Def. Aware costs 82 AP from 65; only the Progressor pays less. Strength costs 82 AP from 65; only the Boss pays less.

The cheap tier holds ten more, priced to 90: Att. Position (71 AP from 80); FK Acc. (92 AP from 75); Curve, Long Shots and Penalties (108 AP each from 70); Aggression, Reactions, Stamina and Volleys (119 AP each from 65); Jumping (126 AP from 60).

What's expensive on a Maestro

Six attributes are in the Maestro's most expensive tier. One is defensive (Stand Tackle); the other five are Agility (177 AP from 75), Acceleration (209 AP from 70) and Dribbling, Finishing and Sprint Speed (234 AP each from 65).

Pace is the big bill: Acceleration and Sprint Speed both to 90 cost 443 AP, 46% of your 962. And past 90 every tier gets steep. On this one, points 91 and 92 cost 20 AP each, 93 and 94 cost 25, then 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 for each point from 95 to 99 — so Agility from 75 to its cap of 99 is 467 AP, 49% of your budget for one attribute.

Maestro vs Magician, Spark, Finisher and Disruptor

Each archetype puts each attribute in its own price tier, so the same upgrade can cost more than three times as much on one archetype as on another. Where the others pay less than a Maestro:

  • Vision: 90 costs 65 AP on a Spark, 129 on a Maestro.
  • Balance: 90 costs 65 AP on a Magician, 151 on a Maestro.
  • Dribbling: 90 costs 65 AP on a Spark, 234 on a Maestro.

How the prices work

Every attribute on every archetype sits in one of four price tiers, and within a tier the price of a point rises with the value you are raising it to. That is why, on a Maestro, the same 15 points cost 65 AP of Short Pass and 177 of Agility: both go 75 to 90, one on the cheapest tier and one on the most expensive. Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest attribute to upgrade on a Maestro in FC 27?

Interceptions and Short Pass: 65 AP each from 75 to 90, the cheapest route to 90 of any Maestro attribute. The Maestro's cheapest price tier is Interceptions, Short Pass, Def. Aware and Strength.

What is the most expensive upgrade on a Maestro?

Dribbling, Finishing and Sprint Speed: 234 AP each from 65 to 90. The Maestro's most expensive tier also holds Agility and Acceleration, and on it every point from 90 up costs 20 AP or more.

How many AP does a Maestro get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars are paid from the same budget.

Which Maestro specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Heartbeat (Agility 92, Stamina 90, Aggression 90): 455 AP from a new Maestro's starting values. Crasher costs 522 AP and Maestro+ 544.

Is the Maestro or the Disruptor cheaper for passing?

The Maestro. Short Pass to 90 costs it 65 AP against the Disruptor's 177, and Long Pass 129 against 151.
