A lobby where you see the build before you say yes

  • Pick the build you are playing and say what you are up for: a drop-in game or a club match.
  • Players in the lobby see you waiting, with your build beside your name.
  • Anyone can ask to join you, with a build of their own or without one.
  • Gamertags are swapped only after you accept.

How the lobby works

  1. Open the build you play and put it in the lobby, for a drop-in game or a club match.
  2. You show as waiting. Your build sits beside your name, so nobody has to guess what you bring.
  3. A player asks to join. If they have a build, you see it before you answer.
  4. Accept, swap gamertags, and meet in the game.

Frequently asked questions

When does the lobby open?

In the next few days. This page will link it the day it does.

Is the lobby for drop-in or for club matches?

Both. You say which one you are up for when you put your build in the lobby.

Can other players see my gamertag?

Not until you accept their request. Until then they see your build and your Pro Clubs HQ name.

Do I need a build to join someone?

No. You can ask to join without one. With one, the player you ask sees what you would play.

Is Pro Clubs HQ part of EA?

No. Pro Clubs HQ is an independent site made by Clubs players. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by EA SPORTS.
