EA match data from 135,000 FC 27 league games suggests the archetypes your humans play together matter. Recycler + Finisher is the best duo: with 4 humans, clubs with it win 47.4% of games and clubs without it 43.3%. Two Sparks is the worst: 40.9% with it, 44.2% without.

The duos that win more

Every outfield duo is in the chart above, best to worst. 7 of the 8 best have a human defender or holding midfielder in them. Recycler + Finisher leads (+3.5 games per 100), then Progressor + Recycler and Progressor + Finisher. Two Finishers is the only all-attack duo near the top.

So if your club is all attackers, your next human helps most at the back or as a holding midfielder. A Recycler behind your Finisher is the easiest place to start.

The duos that win less

Two Sparks wins least: −2.8 games per 100. Spark is in 6 of the 8 duos at the bottom: Maestro + Spark, Boss + Spark, Creator + Spark, Spark + Target, Spark + Magician, Two Sparks.

Magician + Finisher, the most-played duo (86,000 team results), sits in the middle: +0.5 games per 100. With 4 humans it wins 44.4% against 43.6% without.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best archetype duo in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

Recycler + Finisher (holding mid + striker). In EA match data, clubs with it win 3.5 more games per 100 than clubs of the same size without it, then Progressor + Recycler (+3.0) and Progressor + Finisher (+2.9).

Which archetypes should not play together?

Two Sparks wins least (−2.8 per 100 games), and Spark is in 6 of the 8 duos that win least.

Is Magician + Finisher a good duo?

It is the most-played duo in EA match data (86,000 team results) and it wins +0.5 games per 100, a small but clear edge. A defender or holding midfielder alongside the Finisher adds more.

How are the duos measured?

A club's win rate with the duo is compared with clubs that have the same number of humans but not the duo, size by size, then averaged. Squad size is held equal because it matters most: more humans win more. The 90% range comes from re-drawing the clubs many times, so one club's run cannot make a duo.
