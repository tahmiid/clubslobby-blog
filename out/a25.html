Recycler questions

What is the best Recycler build in FC 27?

The Recycler builds people copy most are Rodri '26 WC, Rodri and Joshua Kimmich: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the list at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Recycler specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Thief (Interceptions 90, Def. Aware 90, Stand Tackle 92): 387 AP from a new Recycler's starting values. Recycler+ costs 453 AP and Driver 511.

What is the Recycler's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Intercept. The three specializations carry Anticipate+ (Thief), Pinged Pass+ (Recycler+) and Enforcer+ (Driver).

What is cheap to upgrade on a Recycler?

Its cheapest price tier is Aggression, Stand Tackle, Stamina and Acceleration; its most expensive is Interceptions, Sprint Speed, Agility, Dribbling and Finishing.

How many AP does a Recycler get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.

How tall can a Recycler be in FC 27?

5'8" to 6'4" (172 to 192 cm), at 143 to 187 lbs. The most copied Recycler builds on this page stand 5'8" to 6'3".

Is the Recycler Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled?

A new Recycler starts Controlled. Height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration decide the type: Explosive needs 182 cm or under with Agility at least 10 above Strength, Lengthy needs 183 cm or over with Strength at least 4 above Agility.
