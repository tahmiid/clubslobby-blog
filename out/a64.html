The Disruptor is a midfielder archetype modelled on Roy Keane. The key to nullifying swift opposition counter attacks and creative stars with tireless pressing and timely interceptions. The list at the top holds the FC 27 Disruptor builds people copy most, all at level 40, and the prices and specializations above show what the archetype costs to build. See also what every Disruptor upgrade costs, all 13 FC 27 archetypes, every FC 27 specialization and every FC 27 level 40 build.

What the Disruptor is

A ball-winner first. Its signature PlayStyle is Jockey, its stat spine is aggression, interceptions and stamina, and its ceilings reward the player who reads passes rather than chases them. It replaces FC 26's Engine — same slot in the midfield group, a much nastier job description — with shooting kept deliberately modest. Engine mains will feel at home in the shape and surprised by the teeth.

Building one at level 40

At the level-40 cap you have 962 AP to spend. The specializations all ask for attributes in the 90s, so a level-40 Disruptor picks one identity: Balance/Reactions/Interceptions for Disruptor+, Sprint Speed/Strength/Slide Tackle for Destroyer, or Ball Control/Dribbling/Short Pass for Anchor. Our builds pay the PlayStyle floors first — Bruiser, Intercept and Press Proven all have attribute gates — then spend the rest down the player's real profile.

The job is winning the ball, so the buttons that matter are on the Defending page of the FC 27 controls, animated.

Disruptor questions

What is the best Disruptor build in FC 27?

The Disruptor builds people copy most are Gennaro Gattuso, Aurélien Tchouaméni and Roy Keane: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the list at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Disruptor specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Destroyer (Sprint Speed 90, Strength 92, Slide Tackle 90): 300 AP from a new Disruptor's starting values. Disruptor+ costs 454 AP and Anchor 618.

What is the Disruptor's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Jockey. The three specializations carry Slide Tackle+ (Destroyer), Intercept+ (Disruptor+) and Bruiser+ (Anchor).

What is cheap to upgrade on a Disruptor?

Its cheapest price tier is Slide Tackle, Stamina, Sprint Speed and Crossing; its most expensive is Interceptions, Short Pass, Stand Tackle, Agility, Dribbling and Finishing.

How many AP does a Disruptor get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.

How tall can a Disruptor be in FC 27?

5'6" to 6'2" (168 to 188 cm), at 143 to 187 lbs. The most copied Disruptor builds on this page stand 5'6" to 6'2".

Is the Disruptor Explosive, Lengthy or Controlled?

A new Disruptor starts Controlled. Height, Agility, Strength and Acceleration decide the type: Explosive needs 182 cm or under with Agility at least 10 above Strength, Lengthy needs 183 cm or over with Strength at least 4 above Agility.

Is Disruptor new in FC 27?

Yes — it's the only new archetype in FC 27. FC 26's Engine is gone from the lineup, and Disruptor is its aggressive replacement: same midfield slot, a ball-winner's brief.

Can I make a Disruptor build now?

Yes — FC 27 is in our builder with the game's own numbers. If EA retunes anything in a title update, your builds re-price automatically; nothing you make is lost.
