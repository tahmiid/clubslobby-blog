Two rules sit on top. Key attributes price one tier cheaper — the star ★ in the calculator — and that discount is already inside every number on this page, so never apply it twice. And the tier belongs to the pair, not the attribute: the same stat can be a bargain on one archetype and a tier-3 luxury on another — Agility, Dribbling and Interceptions each price at tier 3 for 12 of the 13 archetypes.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the same attribute cost different AP on different archetypes?

The cost tier is assigned per archetype-attribute pair. An archetype's key attributes are discounted a tier, and the rest follow its overall pricing profile — so Finishing is cheap on a Finisher and expensive on a defender.

Are early points cheaper?

Much cheaper. Below 60, points cost 1–2 AP; in the 95–99 run they cost 14–35. The last four points of a tier-3 attribute cost more than the first thirty.

What about Skill Moves and Weak Foot?

They upgrade on a separate star track with its own pricing, and it is the one part of the cost data where the community sources still disagree — so it is deliberately excluded here rather than published wrong. If you want a concrete target to spend toward instead, the specialization planner prices complete threshold sets.

Where do these numbers come from?

EA publishes no AP table. These prices are recovered from community build planners, agree across two independent sources on every overlapping band, and match five in-game readings checked directly — the same table the Pro Clubs HQ builder runs on.
