How the prices work

Frequently asked questions

Which Pro Clubs archetype is cheapest to upgrade in FC 27?

It depends on the stat. Every archetype has four attributes in its cheapest price tier and they are different on each one: the Finisher's are Agility, Dribbling, Sprint Speed and Vision; the Boss's are Aggression, Heading Acc., Jumping and Strength. Pick the archetype whose cheap stats match your plan; the chart at the top shows all thirteen.

Which archetype gets 90 Sprint Speed for the least AP?

The Finisher and Disruptor: 76 AP each from 70. Of the archetypes that can reach 90, the Target, Creator and Maestro pay the most, 234 AP each from 65.

How much does one attribute point cost in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

Between 1 and 50 AP. On the cheapest tier a point costs 1 AP up to 69 and 35 AP at 99; on the most expensive tier it costs 2 AP up to 60 and 50 AP at 99. Which tier an attribute is on depends on the archetype.

Which specialization is cheapest to unlock in FC 27?

Progressor+ on the Progressor: 211 AP from a new Progressor's starting values (Long Pass 90, Def. Aware 90, Stand Tackle 92).

How many AP do you get in FC 27 Pro Clubs?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars are paid from the same budget.
