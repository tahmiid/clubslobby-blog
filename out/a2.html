Every FC 27 outfield archetype caps each attribute at its own ceiling and starts it at its own value. The highest average ceiling in each category belongs to the Spark for Pace (97.0), the Maestro for Ball Control (98.0), the Creator for Passing (97.5), the Finisher for Scoring (98.5), the Boss and the Progressor for Defending (97.2) and the Boss for Physical (97.3). The Maestro has the highest ceilings overall, and no archetype leads every category.

What the grid shows

  • It splits by position. Every forward’s Defending ceiling (79.8–83.2 on average) is below every defender’s (94.8–97.2), and every defender’s Scoring ceiling (86.5–92.7) is below every forward’s (95.2–98.5).
  • Reactions is 99 on all eleven. Every other attribute has a different ceiling on at least two archetypes.
  • The widest gaps are 24 points, in Curve, Volleys, Stand Tackle and Slide Tackle: in each, one archetype can reach 99 where another stops at 75.
  • The Finisher can reach 99 in the most attributes, nine; the Disruptor and the Marauder in two each.
  • The Disruptor, new in FC 27, is the only outfield archetype that can reach 99 Stamina. Its highest category is Physical (95.8), and it has the least room between start and ceiling of the eleven: 713 points across its 29 attributes, against 777 on the Target.

The ceiling is half of it: where each one starts

A ceiling is how high an attribute can go; the starting value is where a new pro has it before a single AP is spent. Slide Tackle shows how far apart the two can be:

  • Boss: Slide Tackle starts at 75, ceiling 99.
  • Finisher: Slide Tackle starts at 40, ceiling 75.
  • Magician: Slide Tackle starts at 40, ceiling 75.
  • Spark: Slide Tackle starts at 45, ceiling 75.
  • Target: Slide Tackle starts at 40, ceiling 75.

Frequently asked questions

Which FC 27 archetype has the highest attribute ceilings?

Across all 29 outfield attributes, the Maestro: its ceilings average 94.5, the highest of the eleven, and the Boss’s are the lowest, at 91.7. No archetype leads every category, though: the Spark leads Pace, the Maestro leads Ball Control, the Creator leads Passing, the Finisher leads Scoring, the Boss and the Progressor lead Defending and the Boss leads Physical.

Which archetype has the highest Pace ceiling in FC 27?

The Spark: Acceleration 99 and Sprint Speed 95, an average of 97.0.

Which archetype can reach 99 in the most attributes?

The Finisher, in nine: Agility, Reactions, Composure, FK Acc., Att. Position, Finishing, Shot Power, Volleys and Penalties. The Disruptor and the Marauder can reach 99 in two each.

Is the Engine still in FC 27?

No. FC 27 has thirteen archetypes, eleven outfield and two keepers. The Engine is not one of them, and the Disruptor is the one that was not in FC 26.

Why are the keepers not in the grid?

The two keepers carry five goalkeeping attributes (GK Diving, GK Handling, GK Kicking, GK Pos. and GK Reflexes) that no outfield archetype has, so an outfield grid would leave out what they are built around. The head-to-head compares any two of the thirteen, keepers included.
