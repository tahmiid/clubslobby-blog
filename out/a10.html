What the FC 27 ladder does differently

Flip the explorer above to FC 27 and the shape of the climb changes. The cap is 40, not 100, and the whole run is worth 962 AP against FC 26's 3,167 — so a finished FC 27 pro is roughly a third of an FC 26 one, and every point is fought over. You get three PlayStyle slots instead of nine, one signature perk instead of two, and one PlayStyle+ upgrade instead of four.

The surprise is that the early climb is identical. AP earned by level 10, 20, 30 and 40 is exactly the same in both games — 224, 397, 674 and 962. FC 27 is not a slower ladder or a faster one; it is the same ladder, stopped at 40.

The genuinely new thing has no FC 26 equivalent at all: Masteries, at levels 10 and 30, are a permanent progression layer that pays out across every build on your account rather than only the one you are levelling. That is the fourth row in the explorer, and it is why levelling a second archetype in FC 27 is worth doing even if you never play it.

FC 27 numbers are read from the closed beta and can still be retuned before release. Finished level-40 builds and what changed for your archetype go further.

The grind is heavily back-loaded

The match-XP curve steepens hard: level 2 costs 120 AXP, while the single step from 99 to 100 costs 2,740 — 23× as much. Half of the total 137,270 AXP is spent getting past level 68, which means the last third of the bar costs as much as everything before it.

The AP payout leans the same way: you cross half of the 3,167 lifetime AP at level 61, and the final 25 levels pay out 1,123 AP — 35% of everything you will ever earn. A build that needs deep tier-3 attribute runs simply is not finished at level 60, whatever it looks like on paper — what those runs cost is in our AP price guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the level cap in Pro Clubs?

100. The cap was 50 at launch and was raised in a title update — EA's own Pitch Notes still describe the old cap, which is one reason no official table of this exists.

Does levelling up cost AP?

No — it is the other way round. Levels come from match XP and grant AP; AP is only ever spent on attribute upgrades. Nothing on the level track costs anything.

How much AP do you get in total?

3,167 AP across all 100 levels, in per-level awards ranging from 9 to 113. Three independent community datasets reproduce the same total.

When do PlayStyle slots unlock?

At levels 1, 10, 20, 40, 60, 70, 80, 90, 95 — nine in all. Signature perks come at levels 1 and 40, and your archetype's signature PlayStyles upgrade to PlayStyle+ at 30, 50, 75 and 95.
