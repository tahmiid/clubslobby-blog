FC 27's basic controls, page by page

The Button Help menu splits FC 27's basic controls across thirteen pages, and the tabs above follow them exactly. Attacking – Simple (16 entries) is the bread and butter — ground pass, lob, through ball, every shot type from finesse to the low driven power shot, and the fake-shot family. Attacking – Advanced is the deepest page in the menu at 33 entries: driven and lofted passing, crosses, the flair set, precision passing, player lock, and FC 27's new Trigger Curved Runs and Pass and Follow.

Movement (11 entries) covers sprinting, shielding, first touches and stopping the ball dead; Defending (22) runs from jockeying and tackling to teammate contain and the press calls; and Goalkeeper (9) has rushing, diving and keeper movement. Tactics (19 entries) is the d-pad layer — quick tactics, custom tactics and suggestions, all playable above.

Set pieces take four pages: free kicks basic and advanced, corners and throw-ins, and penalties. This is where FC 27 actually changed: the set-piece tactics system puts pre-set player instructions on D-pad up at free kicks and corners — add or remove a runner, send them near or far post, switch zonal and player marking — and the corner-tactics menu itself moved from D-pad down to D-pad up. Throw-ins gain Shielding and Avoidance. The three Be A Pro pages at the end cover playing a single position: calling for passes and crosses off the ball, and the goalkeeper's own defending controls — pages most control lists skip entirely.

Everything else carries over from FC 26 input for input. The complete year-over-year list — every addition, rename and rebind — is in what changed in FC 27's controls, and the other two lists cover every skill move and every celebration. The FC 27 controls hub ties the three together the way the game's own menu does.
