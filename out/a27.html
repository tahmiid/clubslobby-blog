Why the Creator

Capable of delivering precise and incisive passes that can dismantle even the most organised backlines. Nine ceilings hit 99, and the telling ones are Curve, Crossing, Long Shots and Att. Position — delivery stats the Maestro doesn't max. This is the higher, sharper of the two playmakers: less interested in controlling ninety minutes, entirely interested in the five passes that win the match. Defensively it is a passenger, and the build should accept that.

The perks are both about pass trajectory. Grasshopper Passer — lofted through passes gain accuracy and drop perfectly into stride. Bullseye Passer — driven passes hit their target with pinpoint precision. If you'd rather run the whole game from deeper, that's the Maestro — see what you'd trade in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The De Bruyne is the assist king: Vision 98, Crossing 97, Curve 97, Long Pass 96 and Short Pass 96, with all nine PlayStyle slots earned by the passing thresholds themselves — and the Creator+ specialization, whose Assistant perk boosts the Finishing, Balance and Ball Control of the teammate your through ball finds. Your striker's stats improve because you passed to them.

The Palmer is the shooter's version: Sniper asks for Finishing 90, Shot Power 92 and Long Shots 90, and Set Piece Wizard adds extreme curve to every dead ball. Fewer assists, more goals, same eye for space.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,058 and 3,096 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the De Bruyne, the Palmer.

Specialization order: Creator+, Architect or Sniper

  • Creator+ (Long Pass 90, Crossing 90, Vision 92 — 372 AP from the floor): The ultimate Creator! Capable of delivering precise and incisive passes that can dismantle even the most organised backlines. Grants Assistant — through passes in the opponent's half give a boost to finishing, balance, and ball control to your teammate.
  • Architect (Crossing 92, FK Acc. 90, Shot Power 90 — 380 AP from the floor): Builds attacks from deep with raking diagonals no one else sees. Grants Quarterback — long switches from deep gain speed, accuracy and boost the receiver's control.
  • Sniper (Finishing 90, Shot Power 92, Long Shots 90 — 485 AP from the floor): Bends the unstoppable ball from any dead-ball situation. Grants Set Piece Wizard — set piece deliveries gain extreme curve and accuracy.

The honest ranking: Creator+ if assists are your currency — no other perk in the game improves a teammate at the moment it matters. Sniper if you take the set pieces and arrive for cut-backs. Architect is for deep-lying quarterbacks — raking switches off FK Accuracy 90 — a beautiful niche that most clubs don't actually need twice. Frequently asked questions

What is the Creator archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Creator is one of the four midfielder archetypes, inspired by Andrés Iniesta. Vision, Curve, Crossing, Long Shots and Att. Position all reach 99, and its perks (Grasshopper Passer and Bullseye Passer) perfect the two pass trajectories that beat back lines.

What is the best Creator build?

Vision 98 over a 96-rated passing set, Crossing and Curve at 97, and Long Shots 96 to keep defences honest. The full level-100 build costs 3,058 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Which Creator specialization should I take?

Creator+ if you play the killer pass — its Assistant perk boosts the teammate receiving your through ball. Sniper if you shoot and take set pieces. Architect for deep switch-play quarterbacking.

How much AP does a full Creator build cost?

3,058 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Creator+ criteria cost 258 AP beyond the passing core this build buys anyway.

Is the Creator good as a CAM?

It is the CAM archetype for pass-first players — Att. Position 99 and the delivery ceilings live exactly in that zone. If your CAM game is dribbling at defenders instead, the Magician suits you better; if it is arriving to shoot, look at the Maestro’s Crasher spec.

Does the Creator carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Creator you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
