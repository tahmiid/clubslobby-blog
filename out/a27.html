Frequently asked questions

What is the best Creator build in FC 27?

The Creator builds people copy most are Zinedine Zidane, Arda Güler and Dead Ball Demon: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Creator specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Creator+ (Vision 92, Crossing 90, Long Pass 90): 308 AP from a new Creator's starting values. Architect costs 394 AP and Sniper 550.

What is the Creator's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Incisive Pass.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Creator?

Its cheapest price tier is Ball Control, Long Pass, Reactions and Agility; its most expensive is Dribbling, Short Pass, Interceptions, Acceleration, Finishing, Sprint Speed and Stand Tackle.

How many AP does a Creator get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
