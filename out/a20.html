What's cheap and what's expensive

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Sweeper Keeper build in FC 27?

The Sweeper Keeper builds people copy most are Sweeper Libero, Manuel Neuer and Edwin van der Sar: every one a finished level-40 build you can open from the grid at the top and copy into your own club, then change any attribute.

Which Sweeper Keeper specialization is cheapest to unlock?

Launcher (Long Pass 90, GK Kicking 92, GK Reflexes 90): 275 AP from a new Sweeper Keeper's starting values. Sweeper keeper+ costs 289 AP and Extra 617.

What is the Sweeper Keeper's signature PlayStyle in FC 27?

Footwork.

What is cheap to upgrade on a Sweeper Keeper?

Its cheapest price tier is GK Kicking, Long Pass, Vision and Ball Control; its most expensive is Finishing, Dribbling, Acceleration, GK Pos., Interceptions, Stand Tackle, Sprint Speed, Agility, GK Handling and Short Pass.

How many AP does a Sweeper Keeper get in FC 27?

962 AP at level 40, the FC 27 level cap. Skill move and weak foot stars come out of the same budget.
