Why the Sweeper Keeper

A modern-day keeper, comfortable with the ball at their feet, and with a high defensive line. The catalog backs the billing: all five goalkeeping attributes cap at 99, and so do Short Pass, Vision and Jumping — a passing range no Shot Stopper can touch. The cost is nothing on the save side, which makes this the more flexible of the two keeper archetypes; what you give up is the Shot Stopper's rebound-oriented perk pair.

The perks define the role. Back Option — positions to receive back passes, improving distribution under pressure. Rush Specialist — faster rushes off the line to clear danger outside the box. If your club sits deep and never plays out from the back, the Shot Stopper serves you better — compare both keeper archetypes in the head-to-head tool, or start from the full archetype guide.

The two builds

The Neuer is the complete article: the entire goalkeeping core at 99, Jumping 99, Vision 92, and the Sweeper Keeper+ specialization — its Eleventh Outfielder perk improves your control and passing outside the box, which is where this archetype earns its name.

The Ederson is the counter-attack build: Launcher takes GK Kicking to 92 and Long Pass to 90, and Counter Trigger boosts the acceleration of whoever receives your quick release. One save, one kick, and your Spark is through on goal.

Both are public on @buildmaster, both land inside the AP budget (3,118 and 3,138 of 3,167), and opening either gives you a copy to bend toward your own game — the Neuer, the Ederson.

Specialization order: Sweeper Keeper+, Launcher or Extra

  • Sweeper Keeper+ (Vision 90, GK Diving 92, GK Kicking 90 — 417 AP from the floor): The ultimate Sweeper Keeper! Starts attacks like a deep-lying playmaker. Grants Eleventh Outfielder — improved ball control and passing when in possession outside the box.
  • Launcher (Long Pass 90, GK Kicking 92, GK Reflexes 90 — 428 AP from the floor): Turns every save into an instant counter attack with rocket distribution. Grants Counter Trigger — quick releases after a save boost the pass receiver's acceleration.
  • Extra (Composure 90, Short Pass 90, GK Pos. 92 — 559 AP from the floor): Sweeps up behind a high line, defending space no keeper should reach. Grants Libero — defensive actions outside the box gain a boost to tackling and interceptions.

The honest ranking: Sweeper Keeper+ if you genuinely play the libero role — the outside-the-box control is the archetype's whole identity. Launcher if your club counter-attacks; its perk is the only one here that makes a teammate faster. Extra is the niche pick for clubs that defend with a very high line and need real tackling from their keeper. Full pricing across all 39 specializations is in the specialization planner.

Height and weight

The archetype allows 5'10" to 6'6" and 176–220 lb. Go tall but not maximal: reach still saves shots, but this archetype leaves the box, and the last inch costs agility you will actually use. The Neuer stands 6'4", the Ederson 6'2".

Frequently asked questions

What is the Sweeper Keeper archetype in EA FC Pro Clubs?

The Sweeper Keeper is one of the two goalkeeper archetypes, inspired by Lev Yashin. All five goalkeeping stats reach 99 alongside Short Pass and Vision at 99, and its perks (Back Option and Rush Specialist) reward playing out and rushing off the line.

What is the best Sweeper Keeper build?

The full goalkeeping core at 99 with Jumping 99 behind it, then Vision 92 and Long Pass 88 for the distribution. The complete level-100 build costs 3,118 AP of the 3,167 available, and you can open it directly in the Pro Clubs HQ builder.

Sweeper Keeper or Shot Stopper — which should I pick?

Shot Stopper if your job is purely saves; Sweeper Keeper if your club plays a high line or builds from the back. The save ceilings are identical — what changes is everything with your feet.

How much AP does a full Sweeper Keeper build cost?

3,118 AP for the complete level-100 build — inside the 3,167 AP a pro earns reaching level 100. The three Sweeper Keeper+ criteria alone cost 258 AP on top of the save core.

Which Sweeper Keeper specialization is best?

Sweeper Keeper+ for the true libero game, Launcher for counter-attacking clubs (its perk accelerates the player who receives your quick release), Extra if your keeper genuinely defends grass outside the box.

Does the Sweeper Keeper carry over to FC 27?

Yes — EA has confirmed all 13 archetypes return in FC 27 and are unlocked by default, with free resets. A Sweeper Keeper you plan now is a head start, not a throwaway.
